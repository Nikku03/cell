# A 64-sample experiment to make the cell model falsifiable

**Status:** protocol, not yet run. **Written:** 2026-10-05.
**Tests:** a prediction committed before this protocol existed — `memory_bank/cell/state.json`,
state fingerprint `6e5ddfa44841962c`, 5,617 predicted gene directions.

---

## 1. Why this experiment and not another

Five independent routes in this repository have ended at the same place: the blocker is a missing
measurement, not a missing method. The most recent and most specific arrival came from trying to
resolve the model's own contradictions with public data and failing:

- `rem/atlas/dyncell.py` propagated direction over the signed network and left **1,169 genes whose
  incoming signs disagree**. The examples are real biology — BCL2 is up from p53 and down from
  NF-κB; CRH is up from the glucocorticoid receptor and down from NF-κB. Both directions are
  correct in different contexts.
- `rem/atlas/context.py` tried to adjudicate them with the encyclopedia's own expression mask. Its
  blocking gate failed: the mask recovered marker TFs in their own cell type 45/89 = **50.6%**.
- `rem/atlas/hpacontext.py` fetched a real matrix instead — Human Protein Atlas, 3,087,080 rows,
  18,906 genes × 154 cell types — and it **passed validation and still failed**. Apparent
  resolution was 99.3%, but permuting which gene holds which expression profile resolved 99.2%.
  The paired win rate was **46.1% against a 50% chance baseline**.

The reason is diagnostic rather than disappointing. The conflicting regulators — TP53, RELA,
NR3C1, MYC, STAT3 — are all broadly expressed. Their contradictions were never about **presence**,
which is exactly why a presence matrix cannot adjudicate them. The variable that decides them is
**activity**, and no reference atlas carries activity.

**The design move:** activity does not have to be measured. It can be *set*.

| regulator | pharmacological switch | effect |
|---|---|---|
| NR3C1 (glucocorticoid receptor) | dexamethasone | activates |
| RELA (NF-κB) | TNF-α | activates |
| TP53 | nutlin-3a (MDM2 inhibitor) | stabilises / activates |

Those three are precisely the pairs that generated the contradictions. **187 of the 1,169
contradictions involve at least two of them.**

## 2. What is being tested

The prediction is already on the record with a git timestamp, so this is a **pre-registered test of
a standing prediction**, not a fit.

- Prediction file: `memory_bank/cell/state.json`, fingerprint `6e5ddfa44841962c`
- Content: 5,617 gene directions produced by knocking down TP53, NR3C1, RELA, MYC, STAT3
- Journal: `memory_bank/cell/journal.jsonl` — every direction cites the edges that caused it
- Scoring set: the **187** contradictions involving ≥2 of {TP53, NR3C1, RELA}

### Pre-analysis exclusion, declared now

219 of 66,641 signed source–target pairs in the network carry **both** +1 and −1 (0.33%) — a data
defect, not biology. Any scoring-set gene whose votes derive from one of these pairs is excluded
before unblinding. Build the exclusion list from the committed network, not from the results.

### The readout genes

All are well-expressed in A549 and individually well-characterised, so a null result cannot be
explained away as poor detection:

```
CDKN1A  JUN   CCND1  CDKN2A  IL6   DDIT3  BCL2   BAX
CAV1    ABCB1 BIRC5  CDK4    CDK6  FASLG  IGF1R  FOS
```
(full 187-gene list generated by `rem/atlas/dyncell.py`; the above are the highest-vote members)

## 3. Design

**2³ factorial on activity state × 8 biological replicates = 64 samples.**

| # | dexamethasone | TNF-α | nutlin-3a |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | – | – | – |
| 2 | **+** | – | – |
| 3 | – | **+** | – |
| 4 | – | – | **+** |
| 5 | **+** | **+** | – |
| 6 | **+** | – | **+** |
| 7 | – | **+** | **+** |
| 8 | **+** | **+** | **+** |

**Cell line: A549.** Chosen because it is GR-responsive (the repo's existing dexamethasone time
course is A549), **p53 wild-type** — required, since nutlin-3a does nothing in a p53-null line —
and has an intact NF-κB pathway.

**Biological replicates mean biological.** Eight independent passages harvested on **eight separate
days**, each day running all eight conditions. Condition is therefore never confounded with batch.
Eight wells of one plate on one day is a technical replicate and will not satisfy the analysis.

Why 8: the deepest experiment in the entire catalogue this repo surveyed has **4** biological
replicates (66 experiments have 2, eight have 3, three have 4). The depth relation `R = 2(bar/ρ)²`
puts 2 replicates at a 1-floor margin only. Eight is the first point in the repo's own sweep that
recovers a >1-floor ladder span, and it doubles the best that exists.

**Randomise** well position within each plate and the order of harvest across conditions.

## 4. Materials

| reagent | working conc. | vehicle | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dexamethasone | 100 nM | DMSO | matched DMSO in all arms, ≤0.1% final |
| TNF-α (human, recombinant) | 10 ng/mL | PBS + 0.1% BSA | carrier-matched in all arms |
| Nutlin-3a | 10 µM | DMSO | counts toward the DMSO cap above |

Also: A549 cells (authenticated, mycoplasma-negative), standard growth medium, RNA extraction kit
with on-column DNase, RNA integrity assay.

**Vehicle matching is not optional.** Every well receives the same final DMSO and the same BSA
carrier regardless of arm, or the control arm differs from the treated arms by solvent.

---

## 5. Timeline, and the one real compromise in this design

The three pathways do not share a response timescale. p53 target induction needs 12–24 h of MDM2
inhibition; GR primary targets peak around 2–6 h; the NF-κB primary wave peaks at 30–60 min.
A single harvest cannot be optimal for all three. The additions are therefore **staggered** so all
three are in a comparable response phase at one harvest:

```
t = −18 h   seed at a density reaching ~70% confluence at harvest; add nutlin-3a (or DMSO)
t =   0 h   add dexamethasone (or DMSO) and TNF-α (or carrier)
t = + 4 h   harvest directly into lysis buffer
```

**Stated limitation:** at +4 h the sharp 30–60 min NF-κB primary wave (e.g. *NFKBIA*) is partially
missed; sustained targets (*CXCL8*, *TNFAIP3*) remain well above baseline. Adding a second harvest
at t = +1 h recovers it and costs 64 more samples. If budget allows, do the 128-sample version;
if not, this compromise is the reason and it is recorded, not hidden.

## 6. Readout

Bulk poly-A RNA-seq. **Direction is the endpoint, not magnitude**, which is what makes this cheap.

- 50 bp single-end is sufficient for differential expression
- **25 M reads per sample** → 1.6 B reads total
- One NovaSeq X 10B lane fraction, or ~2 NextSeq 2000 P3 runs
- Randomise samples across lanes; record lane in the model

Single-cell is **not** required and would waste the budget: the question is a population-level
direction per condition, and 8 biological replicates of bulk give better power per dollar.

## 7. QC gates — blocking, checked before any scoring

**Q1. Did the switches actually fire?** In the relevant single-agent arm, at least **2 of 3**
positive-control targets must move up with FDR < 0.05:

| pathway | positive controls |
|---|---|
| Dexamethasone / GR | *FKBP5*, *TSC22D3* (GILZ), *PER1* |
| TNF-α / NF-κB | *NFKBIA*, *CXCL8*, *TNFAIP3* |
| Nutlin-3a / p53 | *MDM2*, *CDKN1A*, *GDF15* |

**If Q1 fails for a pathway, nothing involving that pathway is interpretable.** Report the failure
and stop; do not reinterpret the arm.

**Q2. Standard QC:** RIN ≥ 8, ≥ 80% reads assigned, no replicate an outlier by PCA beyond a
predeclared distance, no condition–batch confounding in the design matrix.

---

## 8. Analysis plan, predeclared

Write this into the analysis script **before unblinding**, as the repo's convention requires.

**Q3 — FEASIBILITY, and it gates everything.** Cross-replicate agreement on the *direction* of
change must reach **≥ 0.30**. The existing A549 dexamethasone data reached **0.092** against that
same bar (`outputs/loop_vector_field.json`, change_mean).
*If Q3 fails at 8 biological replicates, direction is not measurable in this system, and that is
the decisive negative result of the experiment. Report it as the finding.*

**Q4 — THE TEST.** Sign accuracy of the committed prediction on the scoring set, versus a
sign-shuffled null over the same genes. Bar: the prediction must beat the null by more than the
null's 95th percentile over 1,000 shuffles.

**Q5 — RESOLUTION.** For each contradiction, which regulator's predicted direction the target
actually follows, per activity state. Report per gene; do **not** report a mean over genes.

**Q6 — THE JOINT REGIME.** Are the double and triple states predictable by adding the singles?
Report the additive model's residual. This is the only part of the design that speaks to the joint
degrees of freedom that single perturbations cannot reach (measured at 15.6% pinned at k ≥ 2 and
**0.00%** at k ≥ 20).

**Q7 — what the experiment cannot settle**, written before the data arrives (see §10).

## 9. What each outcome means

| outcome | meaning | next step |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 fails | the switch didn't work; a reagent or timing problem | fix and repeat; nothing about the model learned |
| Q3 fails (< 0.30) | **direction is not measurable here at 8 replicates** | the dynamical route is closed on this system; report it. This is a real result |
| Q3 passes, Q4 fails | direction is measurable and the network's signs are wrong | the model is falsified — the first time that has been possible. Fix the network against the data |
| Q3 and Q4 pass | the committed prediction holds on a pre-registered test | the first validated predictive claim in the project. Scale to the 128-factor design |
| Q6 residual large | the joint regime is not additive | combinatorial perturbation is required, as the record already suspects |

Every one of these is publishable and every one is worth more than further computation. There is no
outcome of this experiment that leaves you where you are now.

## 10. What this does not settle

1. **Three pathways, not the cell.** It adjudicates 187 of 1,169 contradictions. The other 982
   involve MYC and STAT3, which have no clean acute pharmacological switch here.
2. **Activity is set, not measured.** A ligand activates a pathway; it does not tell you the
   endogenous activity state of an untreated cell.
3. **One cell line.** The repo has already measured that cross-cell-line transfer is weak
   (ρ 0.13 across lines against 0.32 within), so nothing here generalises to another line without
   being repeated.
4. **Direction only.** No magnitudes, no rate constants, no time course. This does not make the
   model a simulator; it makes it falsifiable, which is a different and currently more valuable
   thing.
5. **The +4 h compromise** in §5 partially misses the NF-κB primary wave.
