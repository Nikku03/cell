# Quantum biology speculation note

This directory holds a single document, `SYNTHESIS.md`, generated from a session-32 conversation about whether the Dark Manifold concept's "cellular quantum computation" framing has empirical support in published quantum biology.

## What this is

- A literature-grounded synthesis of the published evidence for cellular quantum effects (photosynthesis coherence, cryptochrome magnetoreception, enzyme tunneling, Penrose-Hameroff Orch OR, tryptophan superradiance, Levin bioelectric work)
- An evidence map distinguishing well-supported, contested, and speculative claims
- A modest theory ("local quantum tools") that the evidence supports
- An honest statement that the strong theory ("cellular quantum coprocessor") is not supported by current literature
- Sources for everything

## What this is NOT

- A peer-reviewed assessment
- Based on full paper reads (the sandbox proxy blocked WebFetch on every bio URL tried; the synthesis rests on WebSearch result snippets and training-data knowledge through January 2026)
- A new theory or empirical contribution
- A project deliverable, fact, or production-code change. The cell_sim simulator, regulation YAML, v15/v16 baselines, and all earlier commits are unchanged.

## Why it lives here

Same convention as `experiments/dark_manifold_salvage_followup/`: thinking work that came out of a conversation, written down so it is durable across sessions, clearly marked as speculation rather than measurement.
