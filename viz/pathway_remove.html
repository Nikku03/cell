Remove PPP2R1A — how the 5‑pathway graph changes

The five‑pathway crosstalk graph with PPP2R1A removed (red ×, its edges cut in dashed red). Any complex co‑subunit that loses PPP2R1A is flagged with an amber ring ⚠.

What changes (direct, from the graph). Removing PPP2R1A deletes 6 edges (1 inter‑pathway). Hand‑off: MAPK nuclear targets ↔ WNT: bridges 4 → 3 (PPP2R5D, PPP2CA, PPP2CB, PPP2R1A → PPP2R5D, PPP2CA, PPP2CB). Structural knock‑on (validated, ~1000× complex co‑essentiality): PPP2R1A scaffolds 4 complex(es), so these co‑subunits lose their partner even though their genes are untouched: PPP2CA.
What does NOT change here (the wall). This is the direct / structural rewiring only. The downstream transcriptional cascade is not shown — the graph predicts a knockout's real movers at only ~3% recall (4× enriched, misses ~97%).
