P5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. Isoform COUNTS are not isoform-resolved edges. Knowing TP53 has 9 forms does not tell
     us which one carries any of its 1,310 signed targets. The network is still gene-level.
  2. A UniProt keyword is a curator's label, not a measurement, and 'DNA-binding' covers
     far more than sequence-specific transcription factors.
  3. Reviewed/Swiss-Prot only. Unreviewed TrEMBL entries are excluded by construction, so
     genes absent here are absent from the REVIEWED proteome, not from biology.
  4. Subcellular location is text with evidence codes; it is not parsed into the
     encyclopedia's 12-compartment vocabulary, and no mapping is asserted.
