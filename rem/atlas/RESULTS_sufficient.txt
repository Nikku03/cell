=================================================================================================
WHAT A PATH MUST RETAIN FOR ITS REMAINING EVENT PROBABILITY, AND HOW BIG THAT IS
=================================================================================================
  THE STATISTIC, from the derivation in this module's header:  (d, s_d, wact_prefix)
  -- the level, the current state, and the accumulated time-weighted activity vector. The
  path weight factors out entirely, so two prefixes sharing the statistic merge by ADDING
  weights, exactly. Everything below is about how many distinct values it takes.

=================================================================================================
F0  THE CEILING GATE: IS THERE AN EXACT MERGE UNDER THE ENGINE'S OWN TIME WEIGHTING?
=================================================================================================
  The theorem says no -- a collision would make exp(1/2) algebraic. Made finite here.

     nCtrl   L  level   prefixes  distinct statistics   merge?
         3   4      3       4096                 4096     none
         3   4      4      32768                32768     none
         4   4      3      65536                65536     none
         4   4      4    1048576              1048576     none

  F0: no merge anywhere -- the statistic IS the path under the engine hvec, as the theorem requires.
  CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE REASON THE REST OF THIS MODULE IS ABOUT APPROXIMATION:
  with the engine's geometric weighting there is NO exact merge at any depth or width, so
  the exact representation is the full path set and nothing can be retained more cheaply.

=================================================================================================
F1  SUFFICIENCY, CHECKED WHERE COLLISIONS EXIST
=================================================================================================
  Under a quantised weighting collisions are abundant, so the check is not vacuous: take
  distinct prefixes sharing (s_d, wact) and verify their remaining contributions are
  exactly proportional to their weights.

  quantised hvec at Q = 8: 65536 prefixes collapse to 4096 statistics,
  3840 of them holding more than one prefix.
  worst relative spread of remaining-contribution-per-unit-weight within a group: 7.795e-16
  F1: PASS -- the statistic is sufficient.

=================================================================================================
F2  THE SIZE LAW, IN WIDTH AND DEPTH SEPARATELY
=================================================================================================
  The derived ceiling is n * (Q+1)^nCtrl, independent of L. If the measured count follows
  it, the representation is exponential in WIDTH and FLAT in DEPTH, which is the collapse
  the engine needs. Counted exactly in integer units of 1/Q -- no tolerance.

=================================================================================================
F2b  THE DEPTH CLAIM, DECONFOUNDED: Q FIXED, L VARYING
=================================================================================================
  The derived ceiling n*(Q+1)^nCtrl does not contain L at all, so at fixed Q the count must
  SATURATE as the window deepens. If it keeps growing, the flat-in-depth claim fails and
  with it the whole value of the statistic.

     nCtrl    Q   L          paths   statistics      ceiling    x paths
         3   12   3          4,096        2,744       17,576         1x
         3   12   4         32,768        4,096       17,576         8x
         3   12   5        262,144        5,832       17,576        45x
         3   24   3          4,096        4,096      125,000         1x
         3   24   4         32,768       21,952      125,000         1x
         3   24   5        262,144       27,000      125,000        10x
         4   12   3         65,536       38,416      456,976         2x
         4   12   4      1,048,576       65,536      456,976        16x
         4   24   3         65,536       65,536    6,250,000         1x
         4   24   4      1,048,576      614,656    6,250,000         2x

  THE FIRST VERSION OF THIS VERDICT USED A THRESHOLD I INVENTED -- growth below
  (path growth)^0.34 -- and it returned FAIL on data that does not support a fail. That is
  the invented-bar defect this record already carries once, in K5's base = -1.0. The claim
  has two parts and neither needs a threshold: the ceiling must never be violated, and the
  COMPRESSION must grow with depth.
    nCtrl = 3, Q = 12: over L = 3..5 statistics grow 2.13x, paths 64x, compression 1x -> 45x (rising)
    nCtrl = 3, Q = 24: over L = 3..5 statistics grow 6.59x, paths 64x, compression 1x -> 10x (rising)
    nCtrl = 4, Q = 12: over L = 3..4 statistics grow 1.71x, paths 16x, compression 2x -> 16x (rising)
    nCtrl = 4, Q = 24: over L = 3..4 statistics grow 9.38x, paths 16x, compression 1x -> 2x (rising)

    ceiling violations across all 10 configurations: 0

  F2b: the ceiling holds everywhere and the compression RISES with depth in every configuration.
  WHAT IT DOES NOT SHOW, AND THE FIRST VERSION CONFLATED THIS: the count is bounded
  independently of L, but it has NOT SATURATED at L = 3 to 5 -- it is still filling up
  toward the ceiling. The growth observed is the approach to a fixed bound, not unbounded
  growth, and those two look identical over three points unless the bound is checked. It
  is checked above.
  So the depth exponential the engine has been fighting is an artefact of carrying the
  PATH rather than what the path is for -- but only once the weighting is quantised, which
  F0 established is an approximation and not a reformulation.

F3  THE PRICE OF QUANTISING THE TIME WEIGHTING
=================================================================================================
  Quantising changes the observable. The reference it is judged against is what pruning
  already costs: accuracy.py measured the reported tail still rising at 0.608 orders per
  decade of retained paths, so a price well under one order is free in the only sense
  that matters here.

  exact tail under the engine's own hvec: 1.187971e-102

  AND THE AXIS IS NOT Q. Quantising at 40 returns numerators whose gcd is 2, i.e. the same
  rationals as 20, so a table keyed on Q shows one approximation twice. Keyed instead on
  the EFFECTIVE denominator Q/gcd and the number of DISTINCT SUBSET SUMS of the numerators,
  which is what actually controls the count. Duplicate weight vectors are collapsed.

      Qeff  subset sums   statistics            tail  price, orders   vs a pruning decade
         5            6       10,000   8.059727e-103         0.1685                 0.277
        10           11       65,536   1.002083e-102         0.0739                 0.122

=================================================================================================
F5  WHAT THIS SETTLES, AND F6 WHAT IT DOES NOT
=================================================================================================
  SETTLED. The exact sufficient statistic for a path's remaining event probability is
  (level, current state, accumulated time-weighted activity vector), the weight factors
  out, and merging is exact. Under the engine's geometric weighting the statistic is
  injective on paths -- by transcendence of exp(1/2), not by measurement -- so the exact
  representation is the full path set and there is nothing to gain.
  Under a quantised weighting the count is BOUNDED BY n*(Q+1)^nCtrl, a bound that contains
  no L at all: exponential in width, independent of depth. Measured at 3 and 4 controllers
  the bound is never violated and the compression rises with every extra level of window.
  It has not saturated over L = 3 to 5, so what is demonstrated is a bound independent of
  depth and a compression that grows with it -- not that the count has stopped growing.

  NOT SETTLED.
  1. The engine-width row is the DERIVED CEILING, tested at 3 and 4 controllers, not
     measured at 10. n*(Q+1)^10 is still 1e13 at Q = 14 -- nine orders below the path set
     and far above what fits in memory. This buys a different exponent, not a small
     number, and calling it tractable would be the overstatement this record keeps
     correcting.
  2. Quantising the time weighting is an APPROXIMATION to the engine's observable, not a
     reformulation of it. F3 prices it at one width and one depth only.
  3. multiplier.py measured that the temporal multiplier PAYS -- recent slices weighing
     more is worth real accuracy. Quantising it coarsely is in tension with that result,
     and this module does not test the interaction.
