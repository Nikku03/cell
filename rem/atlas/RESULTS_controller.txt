C2  THE SUMMATION THEOREM -- must hold twice and BREAK once
=================================================================================================
  Scaling every alpha together scales every enzyme together, so J and E scale by that
  factor and the alpha control coefficients sum to 1. This survives metabolite-sensing
  feedback, because h reads x and the fixed point does not move -- my first-run claim that
  it would break was wrong. It must break when the controller senses the PROTEOME, and
  that case is included so the gate cannot pass by returning 1 unconditionally.
