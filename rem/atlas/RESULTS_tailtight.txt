T3  THE DECOMPOSITION: WHERE THE LOOSENESS ACTUALLY IS
=================================================================================================
  total looseness, certificate over the true deficit: 23.43 orders

    term                                           orders
    sampling slack (composecert, ~1.10x)             0.04
    family floor 0.52 n^1.05 m^1.03, m_eff = 4.99     3.59
    THE BOX RELAXATION, by subtraction              19.80
    total                                           23.43

  T3: THE BOX RELAXATION DOMINATES. exactcert measured that term at ten to twenty orders
  independently and showed it is the one thing in this family that is fixable in principle:
  a bound over the REACHABLE completions rather than a box recovers it. Which means the
  four modules that made this certificate valid, composable and adaptive were tightening
  the sampling term -- worth 0.04 orders of the total.

=================================================================================================
T4  WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT, AND WHAT COULD NOT
=================================================================================================
  best conceivable certificate of this family, floor x sampling x true deficit: 6.8681e-92
  observed certificate:                                                        4.2935e-72
  the gap between them -- entirely the box -- is 19.80 orders.

  If the box were fixed, the interval would be 6.97 orders wide instead of 26.77.
  That is still 3.59 orders of irreducible floor, which no member of this family
  escapes -- so even the best version of this certificate bounds the rare-event
  probability to within a factor of about 4,261, not to within a few percent.
  A certificate tight on the TAIL requires leaving the family, which is exactly what
  exactcert concluded and what nothing since has changed.

=================================================================================================
T5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. T1 is exact at four controllers. T2's engine-width comparison rests on backpoly's
     ESTIMATE of the truth, whose error interval is carried but which is not a bound.
  2. The decomposition attributes the residual to the box by SUBTRACTION, using exactcert's
     floor law and composecert's sampling slack. It is a budget that sums to the observed
     total, not three independent measurements.
  3. m_eff is the certificate-weighted mean of levels remaining, which is the right
     summary for a sum dominated by one level but is a summary nonetheless.
