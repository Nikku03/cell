H3  THE MATCHED CONTROL -- AND IT REFUTES H2's HEADLINE
=================================================================================================
  'resolves in >=1 of 154 cell types'   real 1,159   permuted 1,158   = 1.001x

  THAT CRITERION IS VACUOUS AND H2's HEADLINE DIES WITH IT. Permuting which gene holds
  which expression profile resolves essentially the same number of contradictions -- a
  margin of 1 out of 1,159. Among 154 profiles almost any two differ
  somewhere, so 'resolves SOMEWHERE' is satisfied by construction. The 99.3% figure
  above is therefore NOT a result and must not be quoted as one.

=================================================================================================
H4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. Expression is PRESENCE, not activity. A TF can be transcribed and not active, so a
     resolution says which edge CANNOT fire here, not that the surviving one does.
  2. HPA consensus is averaged over donors and studies. It cannot speak to a particular
     experiment, and the contradictions came from a network with no experiment behind it.
  3. The 10%-of-peak value was calibrated on the 17 validation pairs, so those pairs are
     no longer an independent test of it. Held-out markers would be.
  4. AND THE ROUTE IS CLOSED, NOT MERELY UNFINISHED. The conflicting regulators here are
     TP53, RELA, NR3C1, MYC and STAT3 -- all broadly expressed. Their contradictions are
     therefore not about PRESENCE at all, which is why a presence matrix cannot adjudicate
     them and why permuting it changes nothing. The context variable that would work is
     ACTIVITY, and no reference atlas carries activity: it requires measuring the system.
  5. This closes the loop against a REFERENCE, not against a measurement of the system
     being modelled. The remaining gap is the same one four routes already named: a
     perturbation experiment in a named cell type. Nothing here substitutes for it.
