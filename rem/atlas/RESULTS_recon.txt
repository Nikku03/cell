A NUMBER I REPORTED ONE COMMIT AGO IS WRONG BY A FACTOR OF TWENTY-FOUR.  The previous commit said
212 of 5,938 enzyme-catalysed reactions carry a stable nonzero sensitivity, and called that "the
sparsity the whole K(N) argument predicted, counted directly on human metabolism".  That figure
came from the LP duals -- after R3 had ALREADY FAILED on those same duals.  I should not have
quoted it.  Verified by finite difference in both directions, the count is 9, of which 8 are
internal enzyme-catalysed reactions.

The reason is now measured rather than suspected: of 659 reactions carrying a nonzero dual,
644 -- 97.7% -- have EXACTLY zero realised derivative in both directions.  They are degeneracy
ghosts.  Where the realised derivative is genuinely nonzero the dual is excellent, agreeing to a
median relative difference of 0.0048.  So the dual is an accurate instrument that fires falsely
about forty-three times out of forty-four, which is precisely why it must be a screen and never
the measurement.

RANKING ON THE TIGHTENING DIRECTION WAS NOT A DETAIL.  ENO and DRPA have d+ = 0.00000 exactly:
making those enzymes faster changes nothing at all, while making them slower costs 0.061 and 0.020.
Had the list been built on the relaxation direction alone -- the obvious choice, and the one the
dual most naturally suggests -- both would have been ranked as irrelevant.  GAPD is asymmetric the
other way round, losing 0.163 when slowed against gaining 0.063 when accelerated, a factor of 2.6.
For a degenerate LP the two one-sided derivatives are different numbers and only one of them
answers "must I measure this".

THE BIOLOGY IS COHERENT, WHICH IS A CHECK AND NOT A RESULT.  Every reaction on the list sits in
central carbon or energy metabolism -- glycolysis three times, the TCA cycle, oxidative
phosphorylation, the pentose phosphate pathway twice, and NAD supply.  Nothing exotic appears.
That is what one would expect a growth objective to be limited by, so it is evidence the machinery
is not producing nonsense, not evidence that the specific ranking is right.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT LICENCE.  R8 still fires: top-20 overlap is 6/20 under a different biomass
objective and 8/20 under a tenfold richer medium.  There is no such thing as "the list of human
rates to measure" -- there is a list per question, and the question includes the objective and the
medium.  The analysis also holds enzyme abundance fixed, so a cell that reallocates its proteome
when an enzyme slows is outside it entirely, and it covers metabolism only: transcription,
translation, degradation, transport and signalling contribute no reactions to Recon3D at all.
