=================================================================================================
ADAPTIVE PARTITIONS: CERTIFYING A PRUNER THAT CHOOSES USING ITS OWN SAMPLES
=================================================================================================

  7 pruning levels, 10 controllers, n = 1024. sha 9b909319ccc8.
  studying level 2, which carries 98.4% of the composed certificate: 1,023,000 candidates.

  NOTE ON SAMPLE SIZES. The naive procedure needs MORE samples than the cap of 1000: with
  fewer, the rule keeps every candidate it sampled and the dropped side of the sample is
  empty, so there is nothing to estimate from. That is a degenerate configuration rather
  than a small-sample one, and the sizes below are chosen to avoid it rather than to
  flatter the method.

=================================================================================================
P1  THE SUBTRACTION IDENTITY: UCB(TOTAL) MINUS THE EXACT RETAINED SUM
=================================================================================================
  T = 4.225322e-72 is the total bound over the CANDIDATE POPULATION being partitioned
  here -- which is this level's mass-dropped set, the population a second-stage pruner
  would re-partition. It is fixed whatever the pruner then decides, and that is the only
  property the identity needs. The first version of this line called T the total over ALL
  candidates and then reported a retained share of 0.00%, which was a quantity compared
  with itself; both are corrected.
  a typical adaptive keep of 1000 from 1,023,000 captures 8.65% of T.

       evals   coverage   median UCB/true
       3,000     100.0%             1.089
      10,000     100.0%             1.027
      30,000     100.0%             1.011

  P1: coverage is by construction -- the retained term cancels out of the inequality, so
  the guarantee cannot depend on how the partition was chosen. The evals column includes
  the 1000 evaluations spent on the retained set, which the identity requires be exact.

=================================================================================================
P3  THE IDENTITY'S REAL COST: WHERE THE SUBTRACTION DEGRADES
=================================================================================================
  D = T - R is a difference, and the error in UCB(T) is ABSOLUTE, so the relative width on
  D grows as the retained set captures more of the total bound. That is the regime a good
  pruner is trying to reach, so this is the identity's real limit.

         cap   retained share of T   median UCB/true   coverage
         100                 2.06%             1.061     100.0%
       1,000                13.49%             1.069     100.0%
      10,000                49.05%             1.117     100.0%
     100,000                87.97%             1.488     100.0%
     400,000                98.76%             5.737     100.0%

  P3: the width blows up as the retained share approaches one, exactly as the algebra
  says it must. The identity is free of the selection effect and is NOT free of this.

=================================================================================================
P5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. The identity needs the retained set evaluated EXACTLY. That is cheap when the cap is
     small and it is the dominant cost when the cap is large -- the same regime P3 says
     the width degrades in. Both limits bite from the same side.
  2. It bounds the dropped sum for WHATEVER partition was chosen. It says nothing about
     whether the partition was a good one; a pruner that adapts badly gets a valid
     certificate for a bad decision.
  3. T is fixed only within a level. Across levels the candidate set at level d+1 depends
     on what level d retained, so T at the next level IS random. The composition in P4
     conditions on the realised chain, which is what composecert also did; a guarantee
     unconditional on the whole trajectory is not established here.
  4. The object is still the certificate and not the tail, and exactcert's finding that
     the bound is far looser than the quantity it bounds is untouched.
