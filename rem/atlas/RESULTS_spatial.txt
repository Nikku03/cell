=================================================================================================
  Transcription is nuclear, translation cytoplasmic, with an export step between. My
  expression model collapsed that into one bag. This solves both exactly at matched mean.

=================================================================================================
S6  WHAT SPACE COSTS IN PARAMETERS
=================================================================================================
  One diffusion coefficient per species, which is ~1 parameter per species rather than per
  reaction: about 2e4 for a human cell, against the 4.6e4 kinetic parameters metabolism
  alone already needs. Diffusion coefficients are also far better constrained than rate
  constants -- they follow from size and viscosity within a factor of a few, where kcat
  spans orders. So space is CHEAP to parameterise and expensive to COMPUTE, which is the
  opposite of the rate problem this build order has been chasing.
