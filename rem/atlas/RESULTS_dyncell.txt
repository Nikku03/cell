D4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. It is not a simulator. No magnitudes, no time, no concentrations -- direction only,
     because the record measured that the rate constants do not exist.
  2. 'Unanimous incoming sign' is a rule I chose, not a measured mechanism. Real genes
     integrate regulators non-linearly and this treats disagreement as unexplainable
     rather than resolving it.
  3. The signs inherit the default-activation defect: about half the regulatory signs are
     a rule, not evidence. The sig layer (97.1% signed) is the trustworthy half.
  4. A contradiction names an edge the network cannot reconcile WITH ITSELF. It is not
     evidence against the edge in biology -- no measurement enters this loop anywhere.
     Making that loop touch data is the next step, and it is the whole remaining problem.
