K4  THE VERDICT, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  heuristic range, coverage at 306: 100.0%
  known range (mass only), coverage at 306: 100.0%  -- valid and useless, 1.21e+73x the true sum
  valid AND tight range,   coverage at 306: 100.0%  at 17.18x the true sum

  NOT SETTLED.
  1. One pruning level, one width, one partition. Coverage is a property of the summand
     distribution and that changes with level.
  2. Coverage is measured against the TRUE sum for this partition, which required the full
     scan to know. In use there is nothing to check against -- that is what a guarantee is
     for -- so this validates the procedure, not any particular run of it.
  3. A 95% bound is still not a certificate in the sense exactcert used. It is a
     confidence statement, and the engine's guarantee is demoted by adopting it.
