DO GEOMETRIC RESIDENCE TIMES SURVIVE A NON-EXPONENTIAL GATING STEP?
=================================================================================================
  Strasser, Theis & Marr (Biophys J 102:19-29, 2012, doi:10.1016/j.bpj.2011.11.4000)
  derive geometrically distributed residence times for an all-exponential two-stage model.
  This asks whether that survives Erlang-k waiting on the production step, at FIXED mean
  production flux -- so any movement is attributable to shape, never to flux.

  R-CONTROL  started from the quasi-stationary distribution, exit is exponential BY
  CONSTRUCTION, so CV must be 1. This validates the moment code before any claim.
        k    CV from QSD    |CV - 1|
        1     1.000000000   4.441e-16
        2     1.000000000   1.887e-15
        4     1.000000000   1.554e-15
        8     1.000000000   3.775e-15
     worst |CV - 1| = 3.775e-15   PASS (bar 1e-9)

  R-VACUITY  is the basin a real attractor?
        k=1  stationary occupancy of basin 0.998405  mean residence 1.866349e+03
        k=2  stationary occupancy of basin 0.999752  mean residence 1.261062e+04
        k=4  stationary occupancy of basin 0.999954  mean residence 6.987761e+04
        k=8  stationary occupancy of basin 0.999987  mean residence 2.456200e+05
     PASS (bar: occupancy > 0.9, mean finite and positive)

  R1  ALL-EXPONENTIAL (k = 1): does the memoryless result reproduce?
        exit-time CV = 1.000330   |CV - 1| = 0.000330
     PASS (bar |CV - 1| < 0.05)
     The Strasser/Theis/Marr regime reproduces: residence in this attractor is
     memoryless to within 5%, started from where the circuit actually sits.

  R2  ERLANG-k ON THE PRODUCTION STEP, mean flux held fixed:
        k    mean residence    CV      |CV - 1|   vs R1 deviation
        1      1.866349e+03  1.000330   0.000330       1.00x
        2      1.261062e+04  1.000052   0.000052       0.16x
        4      6.987761e+04  1.000010   0.000010       0.03x
        8      2.456200e+05  1.000003   0.000003       0.01x
     worst departure 1.00x the R1 deviation   MEMORYLESSNESS SURVIVES (bar 3x)

  R3  WHAT IT COSTS -- the mean residence time is the circuit reliability number:
        mean residence spans 1.866349e+03 to 2.456200e+05
        = 2.1193 orders across k = 1 to 8 at IDENTICAL mean flux

  R-ABLATION  Erlang-1 IS the exponential, so the k = 1 row must equal the
  all-exponential row exactly. It is the same computation by construction; recorded so
  that a future change to the Erlang code cannot silently alter the baseline.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
=================================================================================================
  * This is one autoactivating gene, not their two-stage toggle. It tests whether the
    MEMORYLESS PROPERTY is robust to waiting-time shape, not whether their analytical
    expression is right -- it is right, for the model they state it for.
  * Novelty is NOT established. They may already know this. It is sent as a question.
