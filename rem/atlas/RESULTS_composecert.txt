=================================================================================================
Z2  ADAPTIVITY, WHERE THE FIXED-TIME CONSTRUCTION SHOULD BREAK
=================================================================================================
  A genuinely adaptive rule: take a pilot sample everywhere, then spend the remaining
  budget on whichever levels look largest. The sample size at each level now depends on
  data already seen, and a fixed-time bound applied at a data-dependent size is not valid.

    construction        pilot+extra   coverage   median total/true
    fixed-time             100+2000     100.0%               1.199
    fixed-time             300+6000     100.0%               1.073
    sequential             100+2000     100.0%               1.352
    sequential             300+6000     100.0%               1.129

  Z2 AS PREDECLARED: the fixed-time construction covers 100.0% under the adaptive rule;
  the sequential one covers 100.0%.
  THE FIXED-TIME BOUND DID NOT BREAK, AND THAT IS NOT PERMISSION. It is invalid under
  a data-dependent sample size regardless of what it does here; the reason it survives
  is the same slack certcover found, not a property of the construction. Reporting it
  as adequate would repeat exactly the error that made sampcert's 10,039x need
  relabelling.

=================================================================================================
Z3  THE SEQUENTIAL CONSTRUCTION'S PRICE, AND Z4 THE RECOMMENDATION
=================================================================================================

    construction                       valid under                  median total/true
    per-level 95%, summed              nothing -- 65% guaranteed                1.161
    failure budget delta/K             a fixed schedule of calls                1.073
    sequential, delta/K per level      optional stopping, adaptive               1.129

  Z4 THE RECOMMENDATION. The engine's certificate is a SUM over levels, so the failure
  budget composes additively and delta/K per level is the right default whenever the
  schedule of calls is fixed. The moment the engine uses its own certificates to decide
  where to spend -- which is the whole point of an adaptive pruner -- that construction
  stops being valid and the sequential one is required. Its price is the last column.

=================================================================================================
Z5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. The partitions here are MASS-selected and therefore fixed given the chain. A pruner
     that selected using the sampled certificates would make the partitions themselves
     random, and nothing here covers that -- the sequential bound handles adaptive SAMPLE
     SIZES, not adaptive PARTITIONS.
  2. Coverage is measured against the true summed bound, which needed a full scan per
     level to know. This validates the composition, not any run of it.
  3. The object composed is the certificate, not the tail. exactcert's finding stands:
     the bound being composed is far looser than the quantity it bounds.
