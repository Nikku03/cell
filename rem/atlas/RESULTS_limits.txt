L4  WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
=================================================================================================
  1. Parity is a clean test of compositional learning. It is not reasoning, and a model's
     limit on parity says nothing direct about ARC.
  2. Limits are at a fixed 10,000-step budget, identical for every model. A model that
     breaks at k might pass with more steps; so might its controls.
  3. The trees take their branching (4) and depth (4) from Blue Brain morphology. Other
     morphologies were not swept.
  4. Three seeds per cell; the 0.90 threshold is applied to the mean.
