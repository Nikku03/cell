=================================================================================================
THE FOURTH OBSTRUCTION: THE CONTROLLER BLOCK
=================================================================================================
  P_hat(x) = sum_a P(a) prod_i P(x_i | x_pa(i), a). As a flat table P(a) costs
  2^(|C|(L+1)) -- 2^1098 at |C|=122, L=8 -- and that is why trrust_engine's cheapest
  configuration at every L is |C| = 1. tail-legal threshold MI < 2.443e-07

  test system: 3 interacting controllers driving 5 targets, 256 states,
  residual 9.1e-15, block strata 512 = 2^(3x3)

=================================================================================================
E1  IDENTITY
=================================================================================================
  block strata sum to 1.00000000000000
  with controller coupling switched off, max controller-pair history MI = 0.000e+00   PASS
  E1: PASS

=================================================================================================
E2  IS THE TEMPORAL (DBN) FACTORISATION EXACT?
=================================================================================================
  It is exact iff the controllers' sampled joint trajectory is Markov in themselves. That
  holds iff the controllers are an AUTONOMOUS subsystem. Feedback from targets is the knob.
     target->controller feedback   I(a_2;a_0|a_1)      x tau                verdict
                            0.00       1.2563e-17        0.0     EXACT (autonomous)
                            0.05       2.5491e-07        1.0          approximation
                            0.20       4.0027e-06       16.4          approximation
                            0.50       2.6095e-05      106.8          approximation
                            1.00       1.1326e-04      463.5          approximation
                            2.00       4.3671e-04     1787.3          approximation
  E2: the temporal factorisation is an IDENTITY when the controllers are autonomous and an
  approximation otherwise, with error rising steeply in the feedback strength. On the
  closed circuit of circuit.py, where protein feeds back onto the kinase, the same
  statistic runs 288 to 2,273 times tau. So this prong is conditional, not free.

=================================================================================================
E3  CAN THE BLOCK BE DELETED ENTIRELY?
=================================================================================================
  The block exists ONLY because controllers are correlated with each other. If their
  histories were independent the sum would separate and there would be no block at all.
     controller coupling   max pair-history MI      x tau
                    0.00            0.0000e+00        0.0
                    0.05            5.5483e-05      227.1
                    0.20            8.1073e-04     3317.9
                    0.80            9.5093e-03    38917.2
                    1.50            2.4962e-02   102158.5
  E3: NO. Any nonzero controller coupling puts the pair-history dependence hundreds to
  hundreds of thousands of times above the tail-legal threshold. The block cannot be
  deleted; it can only be represented more cheaply.

=================================================================================================
E4  THE CONTRACTION: WHAT EXPONENT DOES IT ACTUALLY BUY?
=================================================================================================
  The engine needs sum_a P(a) prod_i f_i(a_C(i)), a contraction rather than an enumeration.
  Eliminating time-slice by time-slice, the separator between consecutive slices is the
  WHOLE controller slice, because every controller at t has a child at t+1. So:

    representation                                           cost
    flat table                                       2^(|C|(L+1))
    slice-by-slice contraction                   (L+1) . 2^(2|C|)
    sampled                                  S conditional solves

  The contraction is LINEAR in L instead of exponential -- a real and large reduction --
  but still EXPONENTIAL in the number of controllers, because the inter-slice separator
  is the full slice. Slice-internal treewidth does not help that separator. Concretely:
      |C|   flat 2^(|C|(L+1)), L=8   contracted (L+1)2^(2|C|)        ratio
        3                     2^27                    2^6 x 9         2^21
        9                     2^81                   2^18 x 9         2^63
       15                    2^135                   2^30 x 9        2^105
       20                    2^180                   2^40 x 9        2^140
       30                    2^270                   2^60 x 9        2^210
       76                    2^684                  2^152 x 9        2^532
      122                   2^1098                  2^244 x 9        2^854

=================================================================================================
E5  SAMPLING THE BLOCK, with the samples COUNTED as cost
=================================================================================================
     strata used      mode   signed err in Var(targets ON)
             512     exact                       6.550e-16
               1     trunc                      -1.684e-01   <- baseline that must FAIL
               8    sample       -1.449e-02 +- 3.390e-02   (8 seeds)
              32    sample        3.346e-04 +- 1.665e-02   (8 seeds)
             128    sample        3.181e-03 +- 6.070e-03   (8 seeds)
             512    sample        8.720e-04 +- 4.860e-03   (8 seeds)
               2     trunc                      -1.209e-01
               8     trunc                      -7.151e-02
              32     trunc                      -3.760e-02

  Importance-weighted sampling beats greedy truncation at equal strata count -- 32 samples
  give 3.3e-4 against truncation's -3.8e-2 -- and the 1-stratum truncation baseline fails
  at -1.7e-1 as it must, so the bar is testing something.
  THE PER-SAMPLE COST IS NOT ONE. Each sampled stratum still needs the conditional target
  distribution given that history, taken here from the exact block because on a system
  this small the exact block exists. A deployed engine would need a conditional solve or a
  conditional simulation per sample. The honest claim is S CONDITIONAL SOLVES in place of
  2^(|C|(L+1)) table entries. The exponential in |C| is genuinely broken; the constant is
  not free, and this module does not measure it.

=================================================================================================
E7  THE APPLICABILITY CAP ON THE REAL NETWORK
=================================================================================================
  Measured TRRUST controller-subnetwork treewidths: 2, 4, 12, 24, >40 at |C| = 3, 9, 30,
  76, 159. Against a budget of 1e12 table entries:
      |C|      flat, L=8     contracted   affordable?   edges covered
        3           2^27       5.76e+02          True           11.3%
        9           2^81       2.36e+06          True           21.9%
       15          2^135       9.66e+09          True           28.8%
       20          2^180       9.90e+12         False           32.7%
       30          2^270       1.04e+19         False           39.7%
       76          2^684       5.14e+46         False           57.8%
      122         2^1098       2.54e+74         False           68.5%
  The contraction moves the cap from |C| = 1 to |C| ~ 19, covering roughly a third of
  TRRUST's regulatory edges. That is a real gain and it is NOT a solution: the block is
  still exponential in the number of controllers, and only sampling breaks that.
