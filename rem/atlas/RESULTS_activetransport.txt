=================================================================================================
A1  THE MECHANISM SPLIT
=================================================================================================
                   class   count   share of transport
                 passive    2730                64.5%
        secondary active    1018                24.1%
          primary active     482                11.4%
  internal (one compartment)    6370
  transport total 4230 of 10600 reactions (39.9%)
  criterion: primary active if the reaction carries both atp and adp; secondary active if
  it moves Na+, K+, H+ or Ca2+ alongside the solute; passive otherwise.
  The energy cost IS in the stoichiometry, so mass balance already charges it -- a solute
  cannot move uphill without the ATP or the ion appearing as a substrate.

=================================================================================================
A2  THE FREE-LUNCH TEST
=================================================================================================
  every exchange, demand and sink bounded to zero -- nothing enters or leaves the cell
  reversible transport reactions under those bounds: 2323
  maximum ATP flux through ATPM: 1000.0000
  FAIL -- the model creates energy from nothing (bar: exactly zero)
  This is a known pathology of genome-scale reconstructions, not a bug in this code. What
  matters is whether the results in this build order depended on it, which A4 and A6 decide.

A5  THE FIX AND ITS COST

=================================================================================================
A6  DOES THE WHOLE-CELL CONCLUSION SURVIVE THE FIX?
=================================================================================================
  wholecell.py found translation capacity binding, the proteome budget's shadow price
  exactly zero, and every individual kcat three orders below ribosome speed. If ATP were
  free, metabolism could not bind and that conclusion would follow from the defect.

  growth with loops open  : 370.382723 /h
  growth with loops closed: 325.038065 /h
  Growth changes by 12.2% when the loops are closed, so
  the earlier energy-related results ARE affected and must be requalified.

=================================================================================================
A7  THE MASS-BALANCE AUDIT
=================================================================================================
  reactions with parseable formulas checked: 7863
  elementally IMBALANCED: 152 (1.9%)
  Such a reaction lets the optimiser create atoms: it breaks conservation of matter, not
  merely of free energy.

=================================================================================================
A8  THE DECISIVE TEST -- CAN IT GROW ON NOTHING?
=================================================================================================
  maximum biomass with every exchange, demand and sink closed: 1.202481e+02 /h
  against open-medium growth 370.382723 /h  (32.5% of it)
  FAIL -- the model builds a cell out of nothing
  Set overlap could not have shown this: shared reactions only mean shared central
  metabolism. Growth in a sealed box shows the solution can be MANUFACTURED.

=================================================================================================
A9  THE REQUALIFICATION
=================================================================================================
  with the 152 imbalanced reactions removed:
    sealed-box biomass : -0.000000e+00 /h   (was 1.202481e+02)
    open-medium growth : 1.688875 /h   (was 370.382723, ratio 0.0046)
  Sealed-box growth is removed but open growth changes by 99.5%, so every Recon3D-derived result in this
  build order must be requalified by that amount.

=================================================================================================
Active transport is paid for in the stoichiometry and unconstrained in direction. The first
is why this model is not getting transport for free; the second is why it can still make
energy from nothing. Only the second is a defect, and it belongs to the reconstruction.
