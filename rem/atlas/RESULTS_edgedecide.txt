E2  WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
=================================================================================================
  1. One task family -- parity at k = 12 over 16 inputs -- and one tree shape. A result here
     is about sample efficiency on this task, not reasoning and not ARC.
  2. The deep MLP is ONE conventional baseline at matched parameters. Others -- wider,
     differently regularised, convolutional, transformer -- were not tested.
  3. Ten seeds is ten independent units; the 9/10 bar is strict on purpose.

  runtime 295s

POST-RUN CHECKS ON THE TWO THINGS THAT COULD UNDERMINE E0
=================================================================================================
  THE NINTH WIN IS A TIE. Seed 23's difference is +0.000042. Under the predeclared rule it counts
  as higher, so E0 reads 9/10. Tie-aware (|diff| < 0.005 dropped): 8 wins, 1 loss of 9 decided,
  one-sided sign p = 0.0195. Still passes. The honest headline is 8 clear wins, 1 tie, 1 loss.

  THE BUDGET CAVEAT DOES NOT CHANGE THE VERDICT. Dropping all 7 capped deep-MLP cells from BOTH
  models and recomputing per seed: channel higher on 9/10, sign p = 0.0107, paired t = 3.72.

  BUT IT SHOWS WHERE THE EDGE LIVES. In the capped cells (all at n = 3,000-4,000) the deep MLP was
  still climbing -- train 0.93-0.98 after 30,000 steps -- and on 4 of the 7 its test accuracy
  matched or beat the channel tree's. The channel tree's advantage is concentrated at SMALL n:
     n      1500    2000    2500    3000    3500    4000
     ch    0.585   0.681   0.785   0.871   0.954   0.965
     mlp   0.498   0.611   0.641   0.789   0.869   0.944
  and it is STEP-efficient throughout: never more than 1,500 steps, against up to 30,000 for the
  deep MLP, which still could not fit 7 of its 60 runs.
