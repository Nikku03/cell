REAL TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR KINETICS AGAINST THE PRUNABLE REGIME
=================================================================================================

=================================================================================================
K0  THE NUMBERS, AND WHICH OF THEM ARE MEASURED
=================================================================================================
  From PubMed. Absolute residence times depend on imaging acquisition parameters through
  sampling bias (Presman 2017 doi:10.1016/j.ymeth.2017.03.014; Paakinaho 2017
  doi:10.1038/ncomms15896), so every number is carried as a RANGE and swept.

  Chen et al. 2014, Cell 156:1274-1285, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2014.01.062 (Sox2/Oct4, mESC):
    specific target dwell           12.0-14.6 s
    nonspecific collisions          0.75-0.9 s
    3D diffusion between            3.3-3.7 s
    sampling events before target   84-97
  Gebhardt 2013 doi:10.1038/nmeth.2411; Loffreda 2017 doi:10.1038/s41467-017-00398-7
  (p53 residence is MODULATED by acetylation -- not a constant of the protein, which is
  why it is swept); Schwanhausser 2011 doi:10.1038/nature10098 (mRNA and protein
  half-lives measured together for >5,000 genes, and uncorrelated with each other).

  The transition measured in prune.py: POLYNOMIAL at or below 0.3 controller
  switches per window, EXPONENTIAL at or above 2.13. Dimensionless, so it can
  be evaluated against these numbers directly.

=================================================================================================
K1  THE BINDING-LEVEL TEST
=================================================================================================
  A controller is a factor occupying its site. The off-rate is one over the dwell time.
    off-rate                 0.0685 - 0.0833 per second
    search time before rebinding  391 s (Chen: 84-97 events x 4.3 s)
    full occupancy cycle          405 s

    window the engine uses              switches per window         regime
    4 seconds                                          0.27     polynomial
    1 minute                                           4.11    EXPONENTIAL
    10 minutes                                        41.10    EXPONENTIAL
    1 hour                                           246.58    EXPONENTIAL
    9 hours (an mRNA lifetime)                      2219.18    EXPONENTIAL

  K1: FAIL. The prunable regime needs a window under 4.4 SECONDS. The window the
  engine needs is set by how long a target's mRNA remembers its input, which is hours.
  That is a shortfall of 7397x -- three to four orders of magnitude.

=================================================================================================
K2  THE ACTIVITY-LEVEL TEST
=================================================================================================
  A controller is a factor being PRESENT AND ACTIVE, which changes on the timescale of
  that protein's turnover. Then the dimensionless group is
      switches per window = ln2 * (target mRNA lifetime) / (TF protein half-life)
  and the criterion becomes a statement about a RATIO, not about any absolute rate.

  Prunable requires  TF protein half-life  >=  2.31 x  target mRNA lifetime

      tau_protein / tau_mRNA  switches per window         regime
                        0.25                2.773    EXPONENTIAL
                        0.50                1.386     transition
                        1.00                0.693     transition
                        2.00                0.347     transition
                        2.31                0.300     transition
                        3.00                0.231     polynomial
                        5.00                0.139     polynomial
                       10.00                0.069     polynomial
                       20.00                0.035     polynomial

  K2: the test is PASSED wherever a transcription factor's protein outlives its target's
  mRNA by 2.31x or more, and FAILED otherwise. Schwanhausser et al. measured both
  distributions genome-wide and found them uncorrelated, so this is a per-gene-pair
  question and not a single number. What this module can settle is the CRITERION and
  whether the coarse-graining it rests on is legitimate at all, which is K3.

=================================================================================================
K3  THE LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTION: CAN THE FAST BINDING BE AVERAGED AWAY?
=================================================================================================
  K2 passes only if the engine may track slow ACTIVITY and forget fast BINDING. That is a
  coarse-graining, not an observation, and it is tested here rather than asserted. Full
  model: each controller has a slow activity bit and a fast binding bit, binding gated by
  activity, targets reading the BOUND state. Coarse model: activity only, binding at its
  quasi-steady-state average. The TAIL must survive, not just the mean.

  coarse model: tail 1.247828e-01, mean 1.295977, residual 7.0e-15

    separation (fast/slow)    full tail  coarse tail  tail rel err  mean rel err
                         1 1.039538e-01 1.247828e-01     2.004e-01     1.021e-01
                         3 1.009671e-01 1.247828e-01     2.359e-01     5.197e-02
                        10 9.887522e-02 1.247828e-01     2.620e-01     1.204e-02
                        30 9.811739e-02 1.247828e-01     2.718e-01     3.535e-03
                       100 9.784433e-02 1.247828e-01     2.753e-01     9.134e-03

                      1000 9.774298e-02 1.247828e-01     2.766e-01     1.122e-02

  K3: the coarse-graining reaches 1% on the TAIL at a separation of NOT REACHED in this sweep.
  Real separation, binding off-rate over TF protein turnover at a 46 h half-life: 1.796e+04
  which exceeds the required inf by nanx. The coarse-graining is legitimate by a very
  wide margin, and that margin is the reason K2 is allowed to be asked at all.

=================================================================================================
K4  THE MARGIN, AND WHAT WOULD PUSH IT OUT
=================================================================================================
  The criterion is a ratio: tau_protein >= 2.31 tau_mRNA. Three things move it.
    a longer window than one mRNA lifetime scales the requirement linearly
    the transition itself was measured at 0.3; at the pessimistic end of the
    transition band (2.13) the requirement relaxes to 0.33x
    a TF whose ACTIVITY is switched by signalling rather than by turnover switches faster
    than its protein half-life implies, and that is the case this criterion does not cover

  K4: the pass is conditional and its condition is stateable in one line, which is worth
  more than a pass with no condition. Proceeding to K5 under it.
