C1 FAILED, AND ITS FAILURE OVERRIDES C4's AUTOMATED VERDICT.  At the three connectivities this run
shares with sparse.py the exponents disagree badly:

    c        sparse.py P9      here      difference
    2            0.298        -0.043        0.341
    3            0.355         0.113        0.242
    5            0.507         0.620        0.113

against a bar of 0.08.  Different seeds and a different N grid were expected to shift the number a
little; they moved it by up to 0.34, and at c = 2 the sign flipped.  So the exponent is NOT stable
to those choices, and sparse.py's headline alpha = 0.365 must be treated as poorly determined
rather than as a measured scaling law.  The fit quality here says the same thing directly: at
c = 2 and c = 3 the power-law R2 is 0.053 and 0.155, which is no fit at all.  Four sizes and four
seeds cannot pin an exponent when C6 shows the instance spread running from 2 to 25 at a single
point.

C4's printed reading -- "alpha stays well below 1, the sublinear conclusion SURVIVES" -- therefore
stands on ground C1 has removed, and it is withdrawn as printed.

WHAT SURVIVES, STATED AT THE STRENGTH THE DATA SUPPORTS.  Two points have both a clean dilution
check and a fit worth reading:

    c = 5   alpha 0.620   R2 0.898   dilution +0.099
    c = 8   alpha 0.683   R2 0.990   dilution +0.057

Recon3D's median reaction-reaction coupling is 8.  At that connectivity the exponent is 0.683 with
a good fit and no dilution -- sublinear, but far above the 0.365 reported from the c = 3 sparse
run, and the trend of alpha rising with connectivity that sparse.py's P9 found is confirmed at
higher c even though the individual numbers are not.

C3 is what keeps any of this readable: the dense-gradient control returns alpha 0.97, 0.98, 0.98,
1.03, 1.07, 1.06 across every connectivity tested, so the machinery reliably produces a linear
K(N) for a genuinely dense target at every c.  The separation between roughly 0.6-0.7 and roughly
1.0 is the robust finding; the third decimal place of either is not.

c = 12, 20 and 32 are marked DILUTION-FLATTERED (dilution -0.286, -0.225, -0.411) and their
exponents are not readable, exactly as the dense family's was not.  That is the gate working: the
same artefact that nearly got reported as a flat K(N) is now caught automatically.

THE HONEST SUMMARY.  The exponent is sublinear and clearly separated from the dense control at
every connectivity where it can be read, but it is not pinned to better than roughly a factor of
two, and it rises with connectivity.  At Recon3D's median coupling the best-supported estimate is
alpha ~ 0.68.  Extrapolating that to 4.6e4 metabolic rate parameters is a further two orders
beyond the measured range and is an illustration, not a prediction.
