DIRECTION -- does the reporter decorrelate FASTER or SLOWER than its own removal time?
=================================================================================================
  This is the experimentally visible prediction, and the two models disagree in SIGN.
  model                        tau_p      tau_X     Fano  tau_X/tau_p   verdict
  M1 non-consuming pool        2.000   2.347826  1.53333     1.173913    SLOWER
  M1 non-consuming pool        2.000   2.121212  1.32000     1.060606    SLOWER
  M3 sequestered catalyst      2.000   1.969691  0.89375     0.984845    FASTER
  M3 sequestered catalyst      2.000   1.967536  0.88169     0.983768    FASTER
  M3 sequestered catalyst      2.000   1.985889  0.90586     0.992944    FASTER

  A non-consuming shared driver makes the reporter super-Poissonian (Fano > 1) and SLOWER.
  A sequestered catalyst makes it sub-Poissonian (Fano < 1) and FASTER. The sign of the
  effect is set by whether producing the protein DEPLETES the driver, and translation --
  which holds the ribosome and releases it -- is on the depleting side.
