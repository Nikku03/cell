=================================================================================================
DOES THE EXACT BOUND CLOSE THE CERTIFICATE? AND CAN ANY BOUND OF THAT SHAPE?
=================================================================================================

=================================================================================================
E0  THE CEILING GATE: PRICING THE SUBSTITUTION THAT EVERY BOUND OF THIS SHAPE MAKES
=================================================================================================
  4 controllers, n = 16, L = 4, 1,048,576 paths, 200 targets.
  exact tail 1.187971e-102. Every quantity below is computed on the SAME prefix set at each
  level, so the three ratios multiply to the total looseness.

     level  prefixes   TANGENT/EXACT   EXACT/BESTCOMP   BESTCOMP/TRUTH        total
         1       256       1.099e+00        2.147e+20        3.755e+01    8.855e+21
         2      4096       1.297e+00        7.436e+16        2.046e+01    1.973e+18
         3     65536       2.028e+00        4.860e+10        9.880e+00    9.738e+11
         4   1048576       5.990e+00        1.000e+00        1.000e+00    5.990e+00

  medians across levels: the IRREDUCIBLE step -- best single completion over the
  subtree's weighted average -- is 1.517e+01.

  E0 AS PREDECLARED: this is BELOW the 1e3 bar, so the shape is capable of a useful certificate.

=================================================================================================
E0b  THE CERTIFICATE ITSELF: SUMMED BOUND OVER DROPPED, AGAINST THE ACTUAL DEFICIT
=================================================================================================
  E0's ratios are per prefix. A certificate is the SUM of the bound over the dropped set
  against the deficit dropping them actually caused. Both are formed here on the SAME
  dropped set, once with the exact box bound and once with BESTCOMP.

     level    kept    EXACT cert/deficit   BESTCOMP cert/deficit
         2      1%             9.081e+17               1.304e+01
         2     10%             1.067e+18               1.566e+01
         2     50%             1.496e+18               2.270e+01
         3      1%             2.643e+11               4.514e+00
         3     10%             3.074e+11               5.814e+00
         3     50%             4.220e+11               1.023e+01

  E0b AS PREDECLARED: the exact box certificate runs 2.64e+11 to 1.50e+18; BESTCOMP runs 4.51 to 22.70.
  THE CERTIFICATE IS CLOSABLE, AND THE THING TO FIX IS THE COMPLETION SET RATHER THAN
  THE BOUND'S ALGEBRA. Describing the reachable completions instead of relaxing them to
  a box moves the certificate from 1.5e+18 times the deficit to about 23 times it,
  which clears the 1e3 bar by two orders.

  AND THE LIMIT, STATED WITH THE RESULT: BESTCOMP IS NOT AN ALGORITHM. It is computed by
  ENUMERATING the completions, which is exactly what the engine cannot do at its own
  width. It measures what a bound would deliver IF the reachable completion set could be
  described tightly. Quoting it as an achieved certificate would be the same error as
  reading a loop ceiling as a cap, which this build order has now done three times.

=================================================================================================
E0c  THE SCALING OF THE IRREDUCIBLE FLOOR, SINCE E0b's VERDICT RESTS ON IT
=================================================================================================
  BESTCOMP / TRUTH is a MAXIMUM over completions against their weighted average, so it
  grows with the number of completions. The engine runs at 10 controllers, n = 1024,
  L = 6. E0b's encouraging verdict was measured at n = 16 and holds only if the floor
  does not grow too fast on the way.

  A ONE-VARIABLE LAW IN THE COMPLETION COUNT WAS TRIED FIRST AND IT FAILED, WHICH IS WHY
  THERE ARE TWO VARIABLES BELOW. Fitting the floor against completions-per-prefix alone
  gave exponent 0.538 across WIDTHS and 0.241 across DEPTHS -- the same nominal quantity,
  two different laws. A single variable that yields two exponents is the wrong model, not
  an impossible extrapolation: the floor depends on the state count n and on the number of
  levels remaining m SEPARATELY, and n^m does not capture it.

         n  m levels left   completions   BESTCOMP/TRUTH
         8              1             8            5.043
         8              2            64            9.389
         8              3           512           13.565
         8              4         4,096           16.196
        16              1            16            9.880
        16              2           256           20.463
        16              3         4,096           37.548
        32              1            32           16.525
        32              2         1,024           41.602

    log10(floor) = -0.280 + 1.048*log10(n) + 1.028*log10(m)      R^2 = 0.963, 9 points
    the exponent on n is 1.05: the floor grows very nearly LINEARLY in the number
    of states, and only as m^1.03 in the levels left to complete.

    EXTRAPOLATED TO THE ENGINE (n = 1024, L = 6):
      decisions made at                     m        floor
      the last level                        1        750.5
      two levels from the leaves            2       1530.3
      mid-window                            4       3120.4
      the root                              7       5546.6

  E0c AS PREDECLARED: the floor at the engine's width runs 750 at the last level to 5547 at the root.
  IT STRADDLES THE BAR -- under it near the leaves, over it near the root. Since the
  pruner drops at every level and the certificate SUMS those drops, the shallow drops
  dominate and the total does not close. E0b's verdict survives only for the deepest
  decisions, which are not the ones that matter.

    THE EXTRAPOLATION'S HONEST REACH: n is fitted over 8 to 32 and the engine is at 1024,
    five doublings beyond. The two-variable form is what makes this readable at all -- it
    reduces the extrapolation to ONE well-determined exponent per axis instead of one
    exponent that changed depending on which axis produced it -- but it is still an
    extrapolation five doublings out and should be read as a magnitude, not a value.

=================================================================================================
E1  THE EXACT BOUND AT THE ENGINE'S WIDTH, RUN REGARDLESS OF E0
=================================================================================================
  E0's width is not the engine's, and the question was asked directly, so it is answered
  directly. rank='exact' forms the true per-candidate bound, chunked over parents so the
  parents x children x targets tensor never exists in full.

  10 controllers, 1024 states, L = 6, 200 target rows.

        cap     rank            tail     cert/tail   seconds
        100     mass      1.5395e-99     6.496e+98       0.6
        100    bound      6.1775e-99     2.880e+27       1.0
        100    exact      6.1713e-99     2.875e+27      14.3
       1000     mass      7.3706e-99     1.357e+98       1.0
       1000    bound      2.3093e-98     1.839e+26       1.7
       1000    exact      2.3137e-98     1.810e+26     125.7
      10000     mass      2.6793e-98     3.732e+97       8.8
      10000    bound      7.6642e-98     3.088e+25      10.8
      10000    exact      7.7094e-98     3.027e+25    1080.3
      19531     mass      3.7208e-98     2.687e+97      20.4

    at cap 100: exact is 1.00x tighter than the tangent relaxation.

    at cap 10000: exact is 1.02x tighter than the tangent relaxation.
    at cap 19531: exact is 1.02x tighter than the tangent relaxation.

=================================================================================================
E2  DOES THE TIGHTER BOUND ALSO RANK BETTER?
=================================================================================================
  Nothing guarantees it does. A looser bound can order candidates differently and the
  ordering is what determines the recovered tail.

        cap   tail, tangent     tail, exact   orders, exact - tangent
        100      6.1775e-99      6.1713e-99                    -0.000
       1000      2.3093e-98      2.3137e-98                     0.001
      10000      7.6642e-98      7.7094e-98                     0.003
      19531      1.0567e-97      1.0640e-97                     0.003

  E2: the exact bound ranks essentially IDENTICALLY -- the two orderings agree to within a thousandth of an order.
  That is itself informative: the tangent relaxation gives away orders in the
  CERTIFICATE while barely changing the ORDER, which is why boundprune's 6x-8x
  ranking win did not need the exact bound and the certificate could not be
  rescued by it.

=================================================================================================
E3  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. E0c MEASURES the floor's growth rather than asserting it, but it still extrapolates
     about three decades of completion count beyond the widest enumerable point. The
     DIRECTION is measured; the engine-width VALUE is extrapolated.
  2. BESTCOMP is the tightest bound of the form mass x best-single-completion. A bound
     that distributes the ON-probability over the subtree is outside the family and is
     not bounded by E0's floor. That is where a closing certificate would have to come
     from, and it is not built here.
  3. Certificate / tail is the readable ratio at the engine's width because the true
     deficit is unknowable there. It understates the guarantee's quality when the tail
     itself is far below the truth, which it is.
