S5  WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
=================================================================================================
  1. Parity at k = 12 over 16 inputs. Sample efficiency on parity is not reasoning, and does
     not transfer to ARC by itself.
  2. Fixed 10,000-step budget, identical early stopping for all.
  3. One tree shape (branching 4, depth 4, from Blue Brain morphology).
  4. Five seeds per cell. The sign test pools n and seed and treats pairs as independent;
     cells at the same seed share a parity subset, so p is somewhat optimistic.

  runtime 254s
