THE AFFINE MINORANT OF THE SUM, NOT THE SUM OF PER-TARGET MINORANTS
=================================================================================================
  The per-target chord lets every target be worst at its own x. The sum is worst at ONE x.
  That is the same decoupling error as the old box bound, one level down.

=================================================================================================
W0  THE CEILING GATE: IS F CONCAVE? THE VERTEX ARGUMENT NEEDS IT
=================================================================================================
  chord test over 4,000 random secants of the box: 0 violations of concavity
  (log sigma is concave, an affine argument preserves it, and a sum of concave functions
   is concave -- so this is a check on the implementation, not on the mathematics.)

  W0: PASS -- F is concave, so vertex feasibility implies feasibility on the whole box.

W1  THE VERTEX-LP MINORANT AGAINST THE PER-TARGET CHORD
=================================================================================================
  MY FIRST MAJORANT WAS INVALID AND W1 CAUGHT IT. A minorant's slack F - affine is concave,
  so its minimum over the box is at a vertex and vertex feasibility suffices. A majorant's
  slack is CONVEX, its minimum is interior, and vertices prove nothing -- the mirrored LP
  produced 2,380 violations in 4,000 interior points, exactly as that error predicts. The
  upper bound reverts to the tangent, whose 0.70-order gap was never the problem.

    lower bound                         mean gap, orders   gap at centre  violations
    per-target chord                               3.997           4.694           0
    vertex LP, mean objective                      3.013           3.712           0
    vertex LP, anchored at centre                  3.013           3.712           0

  W1: the anchored LP's lower gap is 1.33x better than the chord's, with zero
  violations at interior points -- validity checked independently of the LP that produced it.

=================================================================================================
W5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. 2^k vertices is fine at ten controllers and is exponential in width like everything
     else here. It does not make the method scale in nCtrl; it removes the DEPTH cost only.
  2. Rigour is modulo floating point, and the LP adds a solver whose tolerances are not
     enclosed. The margin covers the arithmetic, not the optimiser.
  3. Containment is observed only where exact enumeration runs.
  4. One term of one calculation, as before.
