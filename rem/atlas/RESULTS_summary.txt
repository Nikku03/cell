=================================================================================================
K6  WHAT CANNOT BE CLAIMED
=================================================================================================
  1. The sufficiency measurements are on synthetic systems of 2-4 controllers and 5
     targets with invented kinetics. Whether a real promoter integrating several
     transcription factors is count-sufficient is an empirical question about promoters,
     not something these numbers settle.
  2. The cap is a COST statement computed from graph arithmetic. It says the engine could
     be built at that size, not that it would be accurate there -- K3's error is measured
     only at the sizes where exact ground truth exists.
  3. The additive form of the test generator's regulation may itself favour a count
     summary. A generator whose targets respond to specific controller COMBINATIONS -- an
     AND gate between two TFs -- is the adversarial case, and it is not run here.
  4. Nothing here says the count is the BEST low-dimensional summary, only that it is
     sufficient where tested and that identity does not matter.
