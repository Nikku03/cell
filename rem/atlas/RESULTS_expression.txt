=================================================================================================
POST-RUN READING.  Hand-written after the run.
=================================================================================================

THE RESULT, AND IT IS THE OPPOSITE OF METABOLISM. recon.py found 8 rates of 5,938 mattered, a
sparsity coming from the constraint side. Here K grows essentially in proportion to the genome:

    assay regime              free dims/gene    K at 19,900 genes    K/G      exponent
    RNA-seq only                    3                 5,125         0.258     G^0.974
    RNA-seq + proteomics            2                 2,578         0.130     G^0.930
    + ribosome profiling            1                 1,982         0.100     G^0.958
    + mRNA half-life                0                     0         0         --

Expression is a measurement wall. Adding assays lowers the constant -- a quarter of the genome,
then an eighth, then a tenth -- but not the exponent, because every gene keeps at least one free
direction and there are always more genes.

THE ESCAPE IS EXACT, NOT STATISTICAL. The final row is not a small K, it is zero, and it is zero
by the identity in X2 rather than by measurement: once a degradation rate is known the per-gene
rank is 4 and nothing is free at all. So the single highest-value genome-wide experiment for
expression is not more abundance data. It is turnover. RNA-seq, proteomics and ribosome profiling
together still leave a wall; adding mRNA half-lives removes it completely.

That experiment exists. Schwanhausser et al. measured abundance and turnover together for more
than 5,000 genes, which is the assay this analysis says is the one that matters, and their finding
that the two half-lives are uncorrelated is why no amount of abundance data can substitute for it.

=================================================================================================
TWO GATES FAILED, AND ONE OF THEM IS SUBSTANTIVE
=================================================================================================

X1 FAILED, and in the worst possible pattern. The Gamma tail was checked against an exact solve of
the two-stage master equation:

    gene   k_dm/k_dp     Gamma P(<T)     exact CME    log10 error
      0         6.5      8.3883e-07    2.3871e-05        1.4542
      1        32.0      9.7594e-03    1.5507e-02        0.2011
      2        30.8      1.4735e-03    1.8331e-03        0.0948
      3        48.0      1.9521e-01    1.9571e-01        0.0011

The error tracks the timescale separation, and the worst case is the DEEPEST tail: at
k_dm/k_dp = 6.5 the Gamma form understates the true probability by a factor of 28. The
approximation fails precisely where the rare event lives. Genes whose mRNA and protein lifetimes
are within about an order of each other are outside this analysis, and the median separation of
30 in the draw hides a spread that reaches down to 6. Every K above therefore carries a tail model
that is good to about 0.1 orders for well-separated genes and wrong by up to 1.5 for the rest.

X3 FAILED, but as a gate-design error rather than a defect in the quantity. The "analytic"
gradient itself uses an internal finite difference for dP/da and dP/db, so X3 compares one finite
difference against another and the h^2 signature cannot appear -- the ratios came out 3.81, 2.17,
0.26. The two agree to between 1% and 5% across the sweep, which is adequate for a ranking, but
the gate as written could not have passed and should have been stated as an agreement bound.

ONE SEED PER GENOME SIZE, and it shows. At G = 1000 under RNA-seq + proteomics K came out 18
against 71 at G = 300 and 561 at G = 3000 -- a single draw where the starting residual happened to
sit just above the criterion. The exponents are fitted through points with that much scatter, so
they are good to perhaps the first decimal, which is enough to separate 0.95 from the metabolic
case but not enough to quote as a law. connectivity.py's C1 taught the same lesson and it was not
applied here.

WHAT SURVIVES ALL OF IT. X2 is an identity and does not depend on the tail model, the gradient, or
the seeds: abundance assays pin the protein mean exactly and leave both of its factors free, and
only a degradation measurement closes the rank. That is the finding, and the K(G) table is
supporting evidence for how expensive the alternative is rather than the result itself.
