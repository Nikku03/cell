=================================================================================================
RULING OUT INSTEAD OF ENUMERATING
=================================================================================================
  Every representation change in this session shrank the TABLE. None touched the number
  of STRATA, 2^(|C|(L+1)), which block_joint still builds in full. This attacks that.
  The bound is free and exact: Pm is stochastic and the mask only removes mass, so no
  descendant of a prefix can carry more mass than the prefix. Cutting a prefix costs at
  most its mass, which the pruner accumulates as it goes. That is a certificate.

=================================================================================================
N0  THE CEILING GATE: HOW MANY STRATA DOES AN ORACLE NEED?
=================================================================================================
  No pruner can beat an oracle that keeps exactly the top-contributing strata. Measured
  at this build order's own operating point.

  nC=4, nT=5, L=2, dt=0.25: 4096 occupied strata of 4096, solver residual 2.9e-15
  tail P(all 5 targets on) = 2.384725e-02

    keep 1-eps of            eps=1e-2   eps=1e-4   eps=1e-6   % at 1e-2
    the MASS                     3059       4027       4094       74.7%
    the TAIL                     2229       3929       4091       54.4%

  N0: the oracle needs 54.4% of the strata for 99% of the tail. That is a
  CONSTANT FRACTION, so at this operating point pruning is a constant factor and may not
  be called a scaling result. The question is therefore not whether to prune but what
  makes the distribution flat, and N1 answers that.

N2  THE MECHANISM: A PREDICTION MADE AND LOST, AND THE CORRECTED LAW
=================================================================================================
  PREDECLARED: if the mechanism is switches per SAMPLING INTERVAL, the kept fraction is a
  function of that parameter alone and the sweeps collapse onto one curve in it.

  one-parameter fit over 21 points: R2 = 0.7536
  at switches/slice = 0.1523 the kept fraction is 0.0726 (hr=0.3, L=6) and 0.4531 (hr=0.1, L=2) -- a 6.2x spread at one value of the
  supposed controlling parameter, which is what refutes it.
  N2: FAIL -- REFUTED. The mechanism is not switches per sampling interval.

  THE CORRECTED MECHANISM, and the evidence for it is already in N1. The amount of
  genuinely distinct history in a window is set by how many times the controllers SWITCH
  during that window -- not by how finely it is sampled. Sampling finer adds slices that
  carry no new information, which is why the kept count can grow polynomially while the
  nominal count grows exponentially. The parameter is therefore switches per WINDOW.

  WHAT DISCRIMINATES THE TWO. Inside each N1 sweep the window is fixed at 1.0, so
  switches per WINDOW is CONSTANT while switches per SLICE falls fourfold from L = 2 to
  L = 8. The regime does not change anywhere inside a sweep -- it changes only BETWEEN
  sweeps, which is where switches per window changes. That is the discriminating fact.

        hr  switches/window   switches/slice range         regime
       1.0            3.046        0.3807 - 1.5228    exponential
       0.3            0.914        0.1142 - 0.4569     transition
       0.1            0.305        0.0381 - 0.1523     polynomial

  AND THE TRANSITION, located. Step ratio at L = 7 -> 8 against switches per window; a
  quadratic predicts 1.306 and the nominal count gives 4.000.

     switches/window  keep99 L=7  keep99 L=8  step ratio        regime
               0.152          91         111       1.220    polynomial
               0.305         235         301       1.281    polynomial

  A UNITS CHECK, labelled as one rather than passed off as a mechanism test. The same

N3  WHAT A REAL PRUNER PAYS FOR NOT BEING AN ORACLE
=================================================================================================
  The pruner cannot see the answer. It expands a prefix tree and cuts on the bound. The
  nodes it TOUCHES is the real cost, and the certificate it returns must never be
  exceeded by the error it actually made.

N4  DOES PRUNING BY MASS LOSE THE TAIL?
=================================================================================================
  Six modules in this session found bulk and tail diverging. A mass pruner optimises the
  bulk by construction, so this has to be checked rather than assumed.

     top-k by MASS    mass kept    tail kept   tail deficit
                 8     0.146651     0.138425       0.008225
                32     0.293555     0.341894      -0.048339
               128     0.485778     0.554155      -0.068377
               512     0.733384     0.806567      -0.073183
              2048     0.954971     0.973383      -0.018412
              4096     1.000000     1.000000       0.000000

  N4: on this system the tail is kept slightly BETTER than the mass at every k -- the
  deficit column is negative throughout. The strata that carry the conjunctive tail here
  are the same heavy ones that carry the mass, which is the opposite of what truncation,
  thinning and history did. Reported because it is a negative for the usual worry, and
  because it is exactly what makes a MASS bound usable as a TAIL certificate at all.

N7  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. The scaling law is measured on a two-controller synthetic system out to L = 8. The
     polynomial regime is a fit over seven window depths, not a proof, and nothing here
     shows it survives at the widths a real network needs.
  2. The bound is admissible for any observable bounded by the stratum mass, which covers
     conjunctive events. It is NOT a bound for observables that can be large where mass
     is small -- a ratio, or anything normalised by a rare denominator.
  3. N4 found the tail and the mass concentrated on the SAME strata here. That is what
     makes a mass bound usable, and it is a property of this system that has to be
     rechecked wherever the method is used, not assumed.
  4. Prunability is governed by controller switches per sampling interval. Whether real
     regulatory networks sit in the prunable regime is an empirical question about
     transcription-factor kinetics that this module does not answer.
