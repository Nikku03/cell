J5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. The join is gene-level. generxn's Human-GEM strings are left in place and are NOT
     reconciled to Recon3D reactions; J0 says why that would be unsound.
  2. A symbol match is not an identity proof. Recon3D symbols come from refseq_name and
     the encyclopedia's provenance for its own symbols is not recorded anywhere.
  3. Tier 1 means a mass-balanced reaction EXISTS for that gene in Recon3D. It does not
     mean the reaction carries flux, and MODELAUDIT M2 found 60 internal reactions that
     do not conserve elements.
  4. Tier 2's signs inherit the default-activation problem measured in the graphvalue
     and encyclopedia censuses: roughly half of the regulatory signs are a rule, not
     evidence. The 'sig' layer (97.1% signed, 35% repression) is the trustworthy half.
