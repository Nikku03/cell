C6  WHY THE TANH TREE ALTERNATES: SYMMETRY, MEASURED
=================================================================================================
  tanh tree     max |f(-x) + f(x)| = 0.00e+00   -> EXACTLY ODD
  channel tree  max |g(-x) + g(x)| = 3.13e-01   -> NOT odd
  Even-k parity is an EVEN function in +/-1 encoding; an odd network plus one output bias
  cannot represent it. Hence chance at k=2,4,6 and 1.000 at k=3,5.
