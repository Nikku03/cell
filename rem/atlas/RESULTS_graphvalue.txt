  The integer cap hid a factor of 100, which is exactly the failure mode V0 predeclared
  reporting the continuous term against. The reason is the growth rate of the cost.

       |C|   per-gene term    log10

  log10(per) = 0.538 * |C| + 5.06   R^2 = 0.991
  THE EXCHANGE RATE: 0.538 decades of cost per controller, so a cost reduction of
  a factor F buys log10(F) / 0.538 controllers of cap.

  Read far out, at |C| = 40, the rewired graph is 100x CHEAPER than the real one --
  the real wiring is WORSE for this engine than random wiring at matched degrees.

  AND THAT FACTOR DOES NOT TRANSFER TO THE CAP, WHICH THE FIRST WRITING OF THIS SECTION
  GOT WRONG. It set the 100x measured at |C| = 40 beside the 1-controller change measured
  at |C| = 13 and called the gap unexplained. The two are at different points. The ratio
  that governs the cap is the one AT the cap:

    real / rewired at |C| = 13                      1.94x
    local slope, |C| = 10 to 13                   0.355 decades per controller
    predicted cap change  log10(1.94) / 0.355     0.81 controllers
    MEASURED cap change                        1

  which agrees. The 100x at |C| = 40 is a ratio read 27 controllers BEYOND the cap,
  where neither graph is affordable, and it was never going to transfer. So the deliverable
  statement is the small one: at the point the engine actually operates, the curated wiring
  differs from random wiring at matched degrees by a factor of two, worth ONE controller.
  V0's bar was set on the cap before the run. The verdict stands at FAIL.

V3  WHAT A REPLACEMENT EDGE SET WOULD HAVE TO CHANGE
=================================================================================================
  Not the wiring. V0 preserved every degree and moved the cap by one controller, and the
  direction was the wrong way round -- random wiring is CHEAPER than the curated wiring,
  so a sequence-derived edge set helps only if it is LESS concentrated than TRRUST. A
  ChIP-style binding predictor is if anything MORE concentrated, because the promoters
  that attract many factors are the ones it will also predict many factors for.

  What moves the cap is IN-DEGREE, and only in bulk. V1's sweep against V1b's matched
  control at equal edge count:

     genes cut  edges removed   hubs cut   random cut  attributable
             1            113         14           13         1.08x
             2            185         14           13         1.08x
             5            351         16           14         1.14x
            20            861         24           15         1.60x
           100           2079        170           17        10.00x

  V3: one gene is not the cap. widthblock's 'from |C| = 20 upward ONE GENE is
  essentially the entire per-gene cost' is a true statement about the cost's MAXIMUM and
  a false one about the cap -- deleting that gene's 113 excess in-edges moves the cap from
  13 to 14. The hub effect is real but it is COLLECTIVE: at 100 genes the cut is worth
  170/17 = 10.0x over deleting the same 2079 edges at random.
  Note also that CDKN1A's in-degree here is 115; widthblock's 52 was its regulators AMONG
  THE TOP 122 FACTORS, a different quantity, and the two are not in conflict.

V4  THE RANKED USES, AND WHICH OF THEM THIS MODULE HAS PRICED
=================================================================================================
  PRICED HERE, AND REJECTED. Replacing TRRUST's edge set with a sequence-derived one.
  Worth 0.8 controllers at the exchange rate measured AT the cap, 0.355 decades per
  controller, and in the wrong direction. V2 removes the motivating hypothesis too:
  the hubs are the BETTER-replicated part of TRRUST, not the worse. No API key is needed
  to reach this conclusion, which is the point of running it first.

  NOT PRICED HERE, AND NOW THE LARGEST MEASURED LEVERAGE IN THE RECORD. The target
  response sigma(base + gain * wact). realkinetics K5b measured the sensitivity of its own
  reported tail to these two constants and recorded that both were INVENTED:
      base -1.5  ->  -9.37 orders        base -0.5  ->  +8.13 orders
      gain  1.0  ->  -0.80 orders        gain  3.0  ->  +0.63 orders
  Half a log-odds unit on an unmeasured constant moves the answer by eight to nine orders.
  tailtight's whole composed certificate is 26.77 orders wide. So the base constant is
  within a factor of two of the entire certification problem, and unlike the box
  relaxation it is not a mathematical defect -- it is a MISSING MEASUREMENT, which is the
  one kind of gap a predictor can close.

  THE LIMIT ON THAT USE, STATED BEFORE IT IS ATTEMPTED. AlphaGenome predicts expression;
  base + gain * wact is a log-odds for a firing event in a CME. A dose-response in intact
  site count pins the SHAPE -- curvature, saturation point, the ratio of gain to base --
  but not the absolute log-odds scale, which needs a link neither the model nor this
  record supplies. A shape with an unknown offset is still strictly more than invented.

  UNCHANGED FROM alphagenome.py's A0: the joint regime. Single-perturbation data pins a
  median 15.6% of the engine's per-gene joint degrees of freedom at k >= 2 and 0.00% at
  k >= 20, and ENCODE supplies 74 perturbed regulators against whatdata's requirement of
  128. In silico there is no such limit. That gate passed; this one did not.
