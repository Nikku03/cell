=================================================================================================
THE COUNT-MATCHED IDENTITY TEST, IN DESIGNED HUMAN CONSTRUCTS
=================================================================================================
  Kheradpour et al. 2013, Genome Res 23:800-11, doi:10.1101/gr.144899.112, via GEO GSE33367,
  sha256[:32] 803c5fd57e2a817e97cd4d9163bdeb9d. 5418 designed constructs, 10 barcodes each, K562 and HepG2
  with two biological replicates and a matched plasmid input.
  204 enhancer families with >= 6 variants; 203 deliberate motif
  disruptions recovered from the sequences, one per family, so the COUNT IS FIXED AT ONE
  and only the IDENTITY of the disrupted motif varies.

  THE COPY-NUMBER AXIS IS NOT TESTED HERE. The design that varies it -- Smith et al. 2013,
  doi:10.1038/ng.2713, in HepG2 -- is not machine-readable from this environment: not in
  the PMC open-access subset, supplements behind an interstitial that blocks automated and
  browser retrieval alike, and the SRA reads undecodable without the barcode map inside
  those supplements. That gap is real and is restated in M6 rather than left here.

=================================================================================================
M1  THE REPLICATE NOISE FLOOR, MEASURED BEFORE ANY MODEL
=================================================================================================
  The quantity scored is the DISRUPTION EFFECT -- activity(disrupted) minus activity(wild
  type) -- a difference of two log ratios, so it carries more noise than either. The floor
  is the noise in ONE replicate's effect, which is what every number below is divided by.

    cell      disruptions   RMS rep1-rep2   FLOOR (one rep)   spread of effects    S/N
    HepG2             196          0.5565            0.3935              0.6978   1.77
    K562              196          0.8902            0.6295              0.7854   1.25

=================================================================================================
M2  THE ASSAY MUST WORK
=================================================================================================
    HepG2    true spread of disruption effects 0.6400 log2 = 1.63 floors   PASS
    K562     true spread of disruption effects 0.6470 log2 = 1.03 floors   PASS
  M2: PASS -- disrupting these motifs moves activity well beyond the noise, in both cell types

=================================================================================================
M3  DOES IDENTITY MATTER, WITH THE COUNT HELD AT ONE?
=================================================================================================
  Mantel-style and free of any motif database: do constructs whose DISRUPTED MOTIFS are
  more similar in sequence show more similar disruption effects? Null by permuting the
  motif labels against the effects, which destroys the pairing and nothing else.

    HepG2: 196 disruptions, 19110 pairs
      corr(motif similarity, |effect difference|) = -0.0308
      permutation null -0.0011 +- 0.0263   z = -1.13   p = 0.1370
      -> no relation between motif identity and effect

    K562: 196 disruptions, 19110 pairs
      corr(motif similarity, |effect difference|) = -0.1275
      permutation null +0.0002 +- 0.0250   z = -5.11   p = 0.0005
      -> similar motifs give similar effects: IDENTITY MATTERS at fixed count

=================================================================================================
  Group the disruptions by motif instance -- identical wild-type windows are the same motif
  -- and take the between-group variance above noise, exactly as humantransfer H3b did.

    cell      motif classes    floor   between-motif sd              in floors
    HepG2                16   0.3935             0.6571      1.67 [1.67, 1.67]

=================================================================================================
M5  REPLICATION ACROSS CELL TYPES
=================================================================================================
  The two cell types are independent measurements of the same constructs. A result present
  in one and absent in the other is not a result, and both are reported above rather than
  pooled, so the reader can see the replication rather than be told about it.

=================================================================================================
M6  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. IT DOES NOT TEST COPY NUMBER. Every disruption here removes ONE motif from a
     construct that has one studied motif. The question 'do two sites act like one site
     twice' is untouched, and the open dataset that would answer it could not be read.
  2. It measures DISRUPTION, not presence: the effect of removing a motif from its native
     context, which is not the same as the effect of adding one to a neutral background.
  3. The motif classes are recovered from sequence, not from a curated database, so a
     class is 'instances that look alike' rather than 'instances of a named factor'.
  4. Two cell lines, one assay, episomal reporters. Nothing here speaks to chromatin.
