=================================================================================================
WHAT DIED
=================================================================================================
recon.py's central metabolic result is GONE, not reduced. With a properly sealed medium no
individual enzyme capacity binds: growth is limited by nutrient uptake alone, so d(growth)/d(kcat)
is zero for all 5,938 enzyme-catalysed reactions. The eight named rates -- GAPD, ATPS4mi, NMNAT,
AKGDm, ENO, PGMT, TALA, DRPA -- were an artefact of a cell being fed through 246 open boundary
reactions, which created internal bottlenecks that do not exist when the medium is the only supply.
R4 now fails outright and R8's overlap is 0/20 under both variants, so there is no list to quote.

trafficking.py's question is unanswerable for the same reason. The dual screen returned ZERO
candidates -- 0 verification solves -- so there is no sensitive set to split into transport and
internal, and the 39.9%-baseline comparison has nothing to compare. (The run printed "CENSORED"
for this, which was a labelling bug: an empty candidate list is the opposite of censoring. Fixed.)

=================================================================================================
WHAT SURVIVED, AND GOT STRONGER
=================================================================================================
wholecell.py's conclusion is intact and now far more extreme. Ribosome speed scores +0.920381
against a best individual kcat of +0.000005 -- a ratio of 184,076x, where the leaky model gave
1,261x. Translation capacity binds in all eight W7 variants, and the model is MORE responsive than
before, not less: dividing every catalytic rate by ten now costs 42% of growth against 26% in the
leaky version. So "the whole-cell answer is set by allocation, not by any individual rate" does not
merely survive the correction, it is the only thing left standing.

regulation.py's picture is cleaner than it was. At the optimum with a proper medium NOTHING is
enzyme-limited, so the count is 0. Forcing the cell off its optimum CREATES enzyme limitations --
41 and 40 reactions at phi=0.85 under the uniform and random programmes -- while the
flux-proportional programme stays at 0 throughout. That is a more interpretable result than the
20-25 the leaky model gave: a growth-maximising cell on a limiting medium is uptake-limited and has
no internal bottleneck, and a misallocated cell manufactures its own.

=================================================================================================
THE GATE THAT SHOULD HAVE CAUGHT THIS
=================================================================================================
recon.py's R2 exists to check that the medium actually binds. It passed on the leaky model because
it tested two things that were both true while the cell was fed from elsewhere: that growth fell
below the all-open value (755 -> 370), and that glucose sat at its bound. Necessary conditions,
treated as sufficient. The test it needed was the one activetransport.py's A8 eventually supplied
by accident -- seal everything and check that growth goes to zero.

=================================================================================================
A CORRECTION TO THIS FILE: "184,076x" IS NOT REPRODUCIBLE TO THE PRECISION IT WAS QUOTED AT
=================================================================================================
The table above reports "wholecell ribosome speed / best kcat  184,076x". An independent rerun of
the same module on the same sealed model gives 57,552x. Nothing changed but the LP's choice among
alternative optima.

    quantity                          run 1        run 2      swing
    k_elong (ribosome speed)       0.920381     0.920829      0.05%   stable
    best individual kcat           0.000005     0.000016       3.2x   NOT stable
    ratio                           184,076       57,552       3.2x   NOT stable

The numerator is stable to four digits. The denominator is a degenerate tie -- eight kcats share
the top value to six decimal places, and the eight reaction NAMES differ between runs (RE1573M and
ADSK in one, NACHEXA8ly and GLDBRAN in the other). This is the same degeneracy that recon.py's R4
gate was built to catch, appearing in a place no gate was watching: not in a ranked list, but in
the denominator of a ratio quoted from one.

WHAT SURVIVES, stated at the precision the evidence supports: the best individual kcat scores
between 5e-6 and 1.6e-5, and ribosome speed scores 0.92. The gap is FOUR TO FIVE ORDERS OF
MAGNITUDE. That is the claim. "184,076x" implied six significant figures on a quantity whose
denominator moves by a factor of three between solver runs, and it should not have been written
that way. The conclusion -- allocation dominates any individual catalytic rate -- is untouched;
its precision was overstated.

This is ledger S in miniature: a number without its applicability domain. The domain here is the
solver's vertex selection, and inside that domain the last five digits are noise.
