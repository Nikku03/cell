H1 CONFIRMED THE PREDICTION.  Predicted 3.891 orders at 1 kcal/mol from linear sensitivities in
quadrature; measured 4.002 from a fully nonlinear Monte-Carlo, a relative gap of 0.029.  The
predeclared refutation condition -- a measured spread below 1.0 order -- did not occur.

H5 REFUTED MY PREDECLARED EXPECTATION.  The docstring says independent draws are the generous case
and correlated error should be worse.  Measured ratios 0.198 / 0.285 / 0.508 are all BELOW 1, so a
shared systematic bias is up to five times LESS damaging here.  The cause is a sign cancellation
I had the numbers for and did not use: the small-error ratio is |sum S| / sqrt(sum S^2), and this
circuit's signed sensitivities are mu -0.0507, k_kill +4.0891, a -3.3703, b +0.3172, giving
0.9853 / 5.3088 = 0.1856 against 0.198 measured.  The two dominant terms oppose each other.
Flipping a's sign gives 1.4553 instead, so correlation can help or hurt by up to sqrt(n) and the
direction must be computed per circuit.  Full correction recorded in hiderate.py's docstring.

WHAT THE DELIVERABLE MEANS, WITH ITS DOMAIN.  At 1 kcal/mol -- the accuracy alchemical free-energy
methods report on well-behaved systems in vitro -- hiding all four rates and letting chemistry
supply them leaves 20.4% of trials within a factor of ten of the true eradication probability and
5.9% within a factor of two.  These are the INDEPENDENT-error numbers, which H5 now shows are
pessimistic for this particular circuit; under a shared bias they would rise.  They are measured
on one four-rate circuit at one tail depth, and rateneed's N6 already showed the requirement
tightens as the question gets rarer, so they should not be read as a figure for chemistry-supplied
rates in general.

H2 QUALIFIES H1.  26.9% of draws at 2 kcal/mol are pinned at 0 or 1, so the reported standard
deviations UNDERSTATE the spread.  The defensible claim is not "the error is 4 orders" but that
over much of the draw range the answer carries no information at all.

H3 IS THE ONE PIECE OF GOOD NEWS.  Ranking rates by measured sensitivity and by realised damage
gives the same order, k_kill > a > b > mu.  So the cheap linear sensitivity is a valid TRIAGE
signal even where it is not a valid error bar: it says correctly which rates must be measured.
That is what M3-style targeting needs from it.
