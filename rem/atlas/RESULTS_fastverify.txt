VERIFICATION WITHOUT VISITING EVERY ACCUMULATION
=================================================================================================
  Affine functions in the log, with closed-form bounds from concavity of log sigma and
  convexity of log-sum-exp. Box ranges of AFFINE drives are exact, which is the specific
  thing the old box bound got wrong by applying interval reasoning to a nonlinear function.

  V0: inside the box: yes; reaches both extremes in every coordinate: yes.

  contributes no looseness of its own. What looseness there is comes from the chord and
  tangent gaps, not from the box.

V3b  DECOMPOSING THE WIDTH, SINCE THE GATE FAILED ON IT
=================================================================================================
  Two relaxations are in play: the chord/tangent gap on log sigma at the terminal, and the
  decoupled residual spread M in the upper log-sum-exp bound. This separates them.

  TERMINAL GAP ALONE, averaged over 2,000 points in the box: 4.695 orders
    upper exceeds exact log g by 0.722 orders, lower falls short by 3.973

  HOW THE GAP ACCUMULATES DOWN THE RECURSION (mean over states, at the box centre):

     level   gap, orders
    terminal         4.695
         3         4.663
         2         4.646
         1         4.637
         0         4.631

  AND WITH THE DECOUPLED SPREAD M FORCED TO ZERO -- NOT A VALID BOUND, A DIAGNOSTIC:
    width with M      : 4.628 orders
    width without M   : 4.628 orders
    so M contributes    0.000 orders and the terminal
    plus the tangent gap contribute 4.628

  V3b: the term to attack is whichever of those two dominates, and the answer is in the
  two numbers above rather than in an argument about which ought to.

=================================================================================================
V3c  WHY THE AFFINE CLASS CANNOT DO BETTER, AND WHAT THE WIDTH SCALES WITH
=================================================================================================
  THE CHORD IS THE BEST AFFINE MINORANT OF A CONCAVE FUNCTION ON AN INTERVAL. Any affine
  function below log sigma at both endpoints is below the chord at both endpoints, hence
  below it throughout. So the 3.97-order lower-bound gap is not an implementation shortfall
  -- it is the tightest an affine lower bound can be, given each drive's range.
  WHICH MAKES THE WIDTH A SUM OF PER-TARGET GAPS, and predicts it grows with the number of
  targets. That is a scaling law and it is measured rather than argued.

     targets  width, orders  orders per target
          10          0.217             0.0217
          25          0.672             0.0269
          50          1.185             0.0237
         100          2.423             0.0242
         200          4.628             0.0231

    width ~ targets^1.007   R^2 = 0.996
  An exponent near one confirms the width is a SUM of per-target gaps. So this verifier
  meets the one-order gate below about 42 targets and fails above it.
  (The first version of this line exponentiated a natural-log fit with base ten and
  printed 5,448 -- wrong by two orders, in the one number here that says whether the
  method is usable. The data alone locate the crossover between 25 targets at 0.672
  orders and 50 at 1.185, which is what the corrected formula returns.)

  THE ENGINE CARRIES 200 TARGETS, so the affine verifier is short by a factor of
  4.8 on width. The quantity a better representation must attack is the
  PER-TARGET gap of about 0.0231 orders, which has to fall by that same
  factor -- not the total.

V4  THE FLOATING-POINT MARGIN, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
=================================================================================================
  margin 0, float64      : [7.8416531093e-105, 3.3271574224e-100]
  margin 1e-09, float64  : [7.8416530779e-105, 3.3271574357e-100]
  margin 0, longdouble   : [7.8416531093e-105, 3.3271574224e-100]
  float64 vs longdouble disagreement: 3.086e-14 orders on the upper end, 1.167e-14 on the lower
  the applied margin is worth 1.737e-09 orders, which exceeds that
  disagreement by 56294.4x.

  WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES: a margin argument, plus an empirical cross-check in a wider
  format. WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH: machine rigour. That needs directed rounding through
  every operation, which this does not do. The enclosure is therefore rigorous MODULO
  floating-point evaluation, and calling it verified without that qualifier would be an
  overstatement of exactly the kind this record keeps correcting.

=================================================================================================
V6  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. Containment is verified only where exact enumeration runs. At the engine's width the
     enclosure's correctness rests on the construction's inequalities, not on observation.
  2. Rigour is modulo floating point, as V4 states.
  3. The affine representation is coarser than enclose.py's cubic, and the width gap
     between them is the price of dropping enumeration. Whether an intermediate
     representation -- affine bounds around a polynomial centre -- does better is untried.
  4. One term of one calculation. Class-map validity, transfer, and biology are untouched.
