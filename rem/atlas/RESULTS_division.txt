V5  THE STRUCTURAL REFINEMENT: HOW MUCH DOES OBSERVING THE CYCLE CLOSE?
=================================================================================================
  In a dividing cell the protein-removal term is k_dp + ln2/T, so in log-rate coordinates
  x = (log k_tx, log k_tl, log k_dm, log k_dp, log T) its derivative splits between the
  two by their shares: d log(removal)/d log k_dp = 0.3429, d log(removal)/d log T = -0.6571.

             direction    residual, no cycle   residual, cycle watched   closed
       burst frequency                0.9953                    0.7779    21.8%
            burst size                1.0000                    0.7779    22.2%
  Watching cells divide is free, and it supplies the dilution share of protein turnover.
  What it does NOT supply is the degradation share, and that is what remains blind.
