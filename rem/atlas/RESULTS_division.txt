V5  THE STRUCTURAL REFINEMENT: HOW MUCH DOES OBSERVING THE CYCLE CLOSE?
=================================================================================================
  In a dividing cell the protein-removal term is k_dp + ln2/T, so in log-rate coordinates
  x = (log k_tx, log k_tl, log k_dm, log k_dp, log T) its derivative splits between the
  two by their shares: d log(removal)/d log k_dp = 0.3429, d log(removal)/d log T = -0.6571.

             direction    residual, no cycle   residual, cycle watched   closed
       burst frequency                0.9953                    0.7779    21.8%
            burst size                1.0000                    0.7779    22.2%
  Watching cells divide is free, and it supplies the dilution share of protein turnover.
  What it does NOT supply is the degradation share, and that is what remains blind.

MY PREVIOUS CLAIM WAS SIX TIMES TOO LARGE, AND IN THE WRONG DIRECTION. The formula-matched
comparator left the means 13% apart and gave a worst gap of 1.2818 orders, which I recorded as
"steady-state expression models UNDERSTATE the tail". With the comparator's removal rate solved so
the means agree to about 5e-10, the worst gap is 0.2024 orders and there is no systematic
direction at all -- three of the eight genes have the CYCLING tail lower than the stationary one:

So most of what I reported as an effect of the cell cycle was an effect of comparing distributions
with different means. The correction stands in the record beside the claim.

V4 STILL FAILS, at 0.2024 orders against a 0.05 bar, and this residual is real: it is the SHAPE
difference between a cycling and a stationary distribution at identical mean. A factor of 1.6 on a
tail probability is worth knowing and is not nothing, but it is not the order-of-magnitude error I
first reported, and expression.py's numbers inherit a factor of 1.6, not a factor of 19.

V7 IS UNAFFECTED AND REMAINS THE MORE INTERESTING RESULT. Immediately after division the
probability of running below threshold is 1.69x to 4.08x the cycle average. That is not a
correction to a steady state -- no stationary model contains a phase at all, so it cannot represent
a periodic window of elevated exposure however well its mean is matched.

V5 IS UNAFFECTED: watching the cycle closes 21.8% and 22.2% of the two blind directions. Four
fifths of what abundance assays cannot see is still unseen after the cell cycle is added.
