HOW DO WE KNOW A PATH IS IRRELEVANT TO THE TARGET BEFORE DELETING IT?
=================================================================================================
  The pruner ranks by MASS and the engine reports a TAIL. accuracy.py measured the
  consequence: pruning binds by 8.4 decades. This tests the bound that would fix it.

  enumerable width: 4 controllers, n = 16 states, L = 4, 1,048,576 paths, 200 targets.
  EXACT tail by full enumeration: 1.187971e-102

=================================================================================================
B5  DOES THE ADVANTAGE SURVIVE DEPTH? THE SCALING LAW
=================================================================================================
  An advantage that shrinks with depth is not an answer for an engine built to run deep
  windows. PREDECLARED before running.
  B5's FIRST RUN HAD A BUG OF MINE AND IT IS RECORDED RATHER THAN QUIETLY FIXED: the
  path count was written nCtrl^(L+1) instead of (2^nCtrl)^(L+1), so the enumerability
  guard was computing 5^5 = 3,125 for a case that is really 32^5 = 33,554,432, let it
  through, and the module died. The printed path column was wrong by the same factor.
  DEPTH IS NOW ISOLATED PROPERLY: L is swept at FIXED width, since varying both at once
  cannot show what depth alone does -- which is the whole predeclared question.
  AND THE METRIC HAS TO BE THE DEFICIT, NOT THE RECOVERED SHARE. The recovered-tail ratio
  SATURATES: once mass ranking already recovers most of the tail the ratio is squeezed
  towards 1 no matter how much better the bound is, so it measures how easy the case is
  rather than how good the bound is. The MISSED share does not saturate, so the
  comparison is missed-by-mass over missed-by-bound.

     nCtrl   L          paths   top-1%  recovered ratio   DEFICIT ratio
         3   3          4,096   73.25%            2.364           1.362
         3   4         32,768   79.78%            1.424           1.294
         3   5        262,144   82.96%            1.488           1.432
         3   6      2,097,152   85.29%            1.467           1.512
         4   3         65,536   89.04%            1.889           1.646
         4   4      1,048,576   91.11%            1.711           1.819
         5   3      1,048,576   98.47%            1.276           2.083

  DEPTH TREND AT FIXED WIDTH (nCtrl = 3), on the DEFICIT ratio:
    over all 4 depths L = 3..6:  +0.059 per level
    over the last three depths:      +0.109 per level
  BOTH FITS AGREE IN SIGN ON THIS METRIC, WHICH THEY DID NOT ON THE OTHER ONE. On the
  recovered-share metric the L = 3 point was a wild outlier and the fit through it
  said the advantage was DECAYING; on the deficit metric it is not an outlier and both
  fits say growth. That the verdict flipped with the metric is the reason the metric
  had to be argued before the fit was read, and not after.

  B5: the advantage GROWS with depth over the last three points.

  AND THE WIDTH TREND, WHICH MATTERS MORE FOR THE ENGINE. At fixed L = 3 the deficit
  ratio rises 1.36 -> 2.08 from 3 to 5 controllers, and the tail's
  concentration rises with it: the top 1% of paths carry 73.25% at 3 controllers and
  98.47% at 5. The engine runs WIDE and deep, and BOTH trends point the favourable
  way over the range that can be checked. That is the strongest thing this module
  can say about the engine's own regime, and it is an extrapolation from three widths.

=================================================================================================
B6  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. Everything here is at widths small enough to enumerate EXACTLY, which is the only
     way to check admissibility and to know the true tail. The engine runs at widths
     where neither is available; that the bound stays admissible there follows from the
     derivation, but that it stays USEFUL there does not, and B5 is the only evidence.
  2. The bound relaxes the coupling between targets -- each target is allowed its own
     best completion. A tighter bound exists and is not built here.
  3. Admissibility is a property of the bound, not of the engine's implementation. This
     module does not modify the pruner; wiring the bound into engine_budget is the next
     step and is not taken here.
