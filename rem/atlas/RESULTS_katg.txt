=================================================================================================
THE CIRCUIT ISONIAZID ATTACKS: KatG, AND WHY ITS MEAN CANNOT PREDICT RELAPSE
=================================================================================================
  INH is a prodrug activated by the bacterium's own KatG, so the killing rate on a cell is
  proportional to that cell's KatG level. Wakamoto et al. (Science 339:91-95, 2013,
  doi:10.1126/science.1229858) measured that KatG is expressed in stochastic pulses
  'negatively correlated with cell survival', and heritable between sibling cells.

  CALIBRATION, STATED IN FULL. Mean KatG fixed at 12 (an arbitrary copy-number
  scale, absorbed entirely into kappa). kappa = 0.020804 per KatG per hour, fixed so a
  MEAN-FIELD model reproduces the ~5% INH tolerance at 12 h that
  Srinivas et al. report (doi:10.1128/mSystems.01127-20). No other quantity is fitted.

KG5  A FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION ON DATA WAKAMOTO ET AL. ALREADY COLLECTED
=================================================================================================
     Under frozen heterogeneity the survivors are enriched in low-KatG cells by an exactly
     computable amount. For a gamma-shaped KatG law the survivor mean falls as
     E[n] / (1 + kappa*b*t).
        t (h)   exact survivor mean KatG   closed form E[n]/(1+kappa*b*t)
            0                  12.000000                       12.000000
            1                  10.656028                       10.668353
            3                   8.655899                        8.730660
            6                   6.676249                        6.861326
           12                   4.444185                        4.804101
     THE PREDICTION: the mean KatG of the surviving population must FALL during exposure,
     by the amount above, with no change in the shape of its distribution. Wakamoto et al.
     imaged KatG in single cells through INH exposure, so the trajectory needed to confirm
     or refute this is already in hand.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
=================================================================================================
  * The copy-number scale is arbitrary and absorbed into kappa. Only kappa*E[n] is
    calibrated, and it is calibrated to ONE published number.
  * M. smegmatis (Wakamoto, Srinivas) is not M. tuberculosis, and a macaque granuloma is
    not a microfluidic chamber. The circuit is the same; the rates are not measured here.
  * Killing is taken as linear in KatG. That is the mechanism of prodrug activation, not a
    fitted functional form, but it is an assumption and it is load-bearing.
  * Rifampin was given alongside isoniazid in the macaque study. This module speaks only
    to the INH arm; rifampin does not act through a self-supplied activator.
