=================================================================================================
G3b  WHAT THAT ESTIMATE IMPLIES FOR accuracy.py's RANKING OF THE ENGINE'S ERROR TERMS
=================================================================================================
  degrees 2 and 3 give 1.5663e-95 and 1.6033e-95, agreeing to 2.4% -- the representation
  has converged even though its accuracy is uncertified. Taking 1.5848e-95 as the estimate:

    quantity                                           orders
    remaining rise implied by this estimate              2.17
    remaining rise from accuracy.py s slope             10.22
    the class map s whole ladder                         5.08

  THE SLOPE MUST FLATTEN. 0.608 orders per decade held over the two decades it was
  measured on, and extrapolating it 16.8 decades gives 10.2 orders where this estimate
  says 2.17. accuracy.py flagged that extrapolation as a direction and not a value; this
  is the first independent number to put against it, and it says the local slope is local.

  AND THE RANKING REVERSES. accuracy.py's A4b concluded pruning binds because the
  extrapolated error crossed 5.08 orders at 10^12.6. If the total pruning error is
  2.17 orders it never reaches 5.08 at all, and the CLASS MAP is the binding term --
  which is what A4 said before A4b overturned it on the scaling law. A4b was right to
  distrust the point comparison; it was wrong about which way the law bent.

  CONDITIONAL, AND THE CONDITION IS NOT SMALL. This rests on an ESTIMATE that carries no
  bound. Its evidence is G1: 0.012 to 0.10 orders against an exact answer at four
  controllers. Nothing tests that transfer at ten. If the estimate is low, the pruning
  error is larger than stated and the reversal weakens or vanishes. What is NOT
  conditional is the direction: the tail is at least 1.0640e-97 by the lower bound and
  this estimate puts it near 1.58e-95, so the slope cannot continue at 0.608 for 17 more decades.

=================================================================================================
G5  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. G3 has no exact reference. Landing above a lower bound is a consistency check that
     the method could pass while still being far from the truth. It is not accuracy.
  2. The projection of log-sum-exp onto the polynomial space is the only approximation and
     it is unbounded -- nothing here certifies it, so this method produces an ESTIMATE and
     not a certificate. exactcert's problem is untouched by it.
  3. G0 tests smoothness at 4 controllers. That REM's backward function stays smooth at
     ten is assumed by G3 and not shown.
  4. Collocation points are drawn uniformly in the reachable box. A better design would
     weight where the mass actually is, and that is not tried.
