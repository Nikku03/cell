PARTITION THE SHARED ACCUMULATION, PROPAGATE BOUNDS WITH THEIR PROBABILITY ACCOUNTING
=================================================================================================
  One split of one accumulation coordinate narrows EVERY target's drive at once, which is
  why the region count can stay small where splitting 200 drives independently is 2^200.
  The transition operator is applied with the probabilities used EXACTLY; only region
  membership is relaxed, pessimistically on both sides.

=================================================================================================
R1b  THE MECHANISM: THE ACCUMULATION MOVES AS FAR AS THE PARTITION IS WIDE
=================================================================================================
  Region membership is informative only if a region is large compared with how far the
  accumulation moves in one level. It is not.

     level   shift h_d   cut spacing  shift / spacing
         1      0.0956        0.5000             0.19
         2      0.1577        0.5000             0.32
         3      0.2600        0.5000             0.52
         4      0.4287        0.5000             0.86

  A shift comparable to the cut spacing means a shifted region straddles the cut for most
  starting positions, and SPLITTING FURTHER MAKES IT WORSE: the regions shrink while the
  shift does not. That is why the crossings column roughly doubles per split, and it is a
  property of the geometry rather than of the bound carried inside each region.
  It is also sufficient.py's injectivity seen from another side: the accumulation is a
  different point after every step, so coarse membership in it survives no step at all.

=================================================================================================
R3  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. The bound carried per region is a CONSTANT. That is the spec's letter and it is coarse
     in ten dimensions: a piecewise-constant enclosure needs many regions to beat a single
     affine one, and the sweep is where that shows.
  2. Region membership is relaxed at boundaries by taking the extreme over reachable
     regions. That is sound and it is the transition ambiguity the crossings column tracks.
  3. Cuts are at coordinate midpoints, chosen greedily by curvature. Neither the position
     nor the greedy order is optimised.
  4. Rigour is modulo floating point, and containment is observed only where exact
     enumeration runs.
