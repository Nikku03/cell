=================================================================================================
E4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
=================================================================================================
  1. THE VERIFIER COSTS O(PATHS). It is exact because it visits every reachable point, and
     that is precisely why it does not yet scale. The gate is width AT a stated cost, and
     the cost stated here is enumeration. Making the verification cheap without
     reintroducing a relaxation is the next problem and it is not solved here.
  2. These are small instances. Nothing here shows the width stays under an order at the
     engine's width, where the verifier cannot run at all.
  3. The enclosure is of the tail computed with this engine's fixed base and gain. It is a
     rigorous interval for THAT quantity, not for a biological probability.
  4. It does not touch class-map validity, transfer to other instances, or anything
     biological. It addresses one term of one calculation.
