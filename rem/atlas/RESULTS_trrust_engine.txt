T9  WHY IT WINS ON SYNTHETIC TOPOLOGY AND LOSES ON REAL  (added after T5)
=================================================================================================
  engine.py's mixed system gave every target a chain neighbour. Real hub neighbourhoods
  have almost none, so the hybrid degenerates to route 3 alone -- which history.py measured
  as 100x dearer than bounded width for a local question. Sweeping residual density p:
        p  resid edges         route1         route2           HYBRID
     0.00            0     2046 (r=2)     1534 (w=8)      not reached
     0.25            2     2046 (r=2)     2046 (w=9)      not reached
     0.50            4     2046 (r=2)     2046 (w=9)      not reached
     0.75            7     2046 (r=2)     2046 (w=9)      not reached
     1.00            9     2046 (r=2)     2046 (w=9)      not reached

  real TRRUST hub neighbourhoods sit at p = 0.00 (range 0.00 to 0.36)
  T9: real hub neighbourhoods have almost no residual structure, so the r-ball component
  of the hybrid has nothing to do and the engine reduces to route 3, which is the
  expensive one. The advantage measured on synthetic star-plus-chain does NOT transfer.

=================================================================================================
T8  WHAT CANNOT BE CLAIMED FROM THIS MEASUREMENT
=================================================================================================
  1. No accuracy number for the full 2,861-gene network. Only cost is measured there.
  2. Nothing about human gene regulation. TRRUST supplies topology and a sign; every rate,
     Hill form and timescale is invented, so accuracy results are statements about this
     model ON real topology, not about cells.
  3. Nothing about the 51% of edges whose sign is Unknown beyond the spread across the
     two treatments run here. The third, deleting Unknown edges, was dropped for runtime
     and its earlier run showed it removes most of the dependence entirely (an independent-
     genes baseline of 9.9e-16), so it is largely vacuous rather than informative.
  4. No claim that the controller threshold is principled. It is swept; TRRUST's degree
     distribution has no gap, so the threshold is a free parameter and its effect is
     reported rather than optimised away.
  5. Nothing about dynamics, only about the stationary distribution.
  6. The controller/target timescale ratio is set by the invented kinetics, not by TRRUST.
     Measured tau_c is around 0.3 against target rates of order 1-2, i.e. MATCHED -- which
     statedim S6 identified as the expensive regime. A different rate choice would move
     the answer, and that is a property of the model, not a finding about the network.

=================================================================================================
CORRECTIONS FOUND AFTER THE RUN, BY ADVERSARIAL REVIEW RATHER THAN BY ANY GATE
=================================================================================================
Eight independent reviewers were asked, before this module was written, to find ways the
measurement could be invalid. Their consolidated gate list was returned after the run and
recomputed several quantities directly from trrust_human.tsv. Five defects are confirmed. All are
mine. Two of them invalidate half this module.

C1  DUPLICATE EDGE RECORDS -- THE ACCURACY HALF RAN ON A DISTORTED MODEL. CONFIRMED.
    TRRUST lists a (TF, target) pair once per supporting publication: 9,369 non-self records
    collapse to 8,403 distinct directed pairs, so 966 records are repeats. generator() appends one
    regulatory factor PER RECORD, so a pair supported by three papers has its Hill factor applied
    three times. Eleven of the twelve subnetworks solved in T5/T6/T7 contain duplicates -- NFKB1
    at N=12 has 7 duplicate records among 20. The kinetics in those tables are therefore not the
    kinetics the module describes, and every accuracy number in T5, T6, T7 and T9 must be
    recomputed on deduplicated pairs before it means anything.

C2  THE UNSIGNED-EDGE FRACTION IS QUOTED THREE WAYS, TWO OF THEM WRONG. CONFIRMED.
    The docstring says 51%, the printed header says 46%, and T8 says 51%. The correct statement:
    3,558 of 8,403 distinct directed pairs (42.3%) carry only Unknown records; 4,312 of 9,369
    RECORDS (46.0%) are Unknown. The 46% figure divides a record count by a record count and is
    right for records; the 51% figure is right for neither. And 207 pairs carry BOTH Activation
    and Repression from different papers -- a conflict the module resolves by dict-append order,
    which is to say by accident.

C3  THE k_i CENSUS COUNTED CONTROLLERS. CONFIRMED.
    k_i is meant to be the number of CONTROLLER parents of a NON-controller gene. T2 computed it
    over all genes, so controller-controller adjacency was counted as history to carry. At
    threshold 25 the printed max is 53 and T4's endpoint total is 7,033; restricted to the 2,739
    non-controllers the true figures are max 17 and 5,143. The cost formula then charges
    controllers twice -- once in the per-gene term and again in the block.

C4  THE DETECTOR USES TOTAL DEGREE WHILE THE PROSE SAYS OUT-DEGREE. CONFIRMED.
    The top 122 by total degree and by out-degree differ by 25 genes, and 21 genes in the
    total-degree set have OUT-DEGREE ZERO -- they regulate nothing and cannot be controllers of
    anything. The claim that removing controllers collapses the maximum residual 2-ball to 96 holds
    under the total-degree detector; under a directed detector at the same |C| it is 285, against
    1,813 with no removal.

C5  TWO FIGURES IN THE RETRACTION TABLE DO NOT REPRODUCE. CONFIRMED.
    RESULTS_TWOPROBLEMS.txt quotes corrected per-gene costs of 1.839e+55 and 9.503e+55 at |C| = 38
    and 122. This module's own T1, computing the same formula, gives 5.521e+70 and 1.323e+121.
    The retraction's DIRECTION is right and its conclusion is unchanged -- the engine.py cost was
    wrong by scores of orders of magnitude either way -- but those two numbers were computed with
    the all-gene k vector and should be read from T1 instead.

WHAT SURVIVES THIS.
    The retraction of engine.py's genome-scale figure stands: history bits multiply across
    controllers rather than adding, and the controller block was priced at zero. Both are
    structural and neither depends on the arithmetic above.
    T1's finding that the corrected functional is non-degenerate stands.
    T3's finding stands and is the sharpest result here: the cheapest configuration at EVERY L is
    |C| = 1. The engine is affordable on the real network only when almost nothing is designated a
    controller, because the controller block 2^(|C|(L+1)) is exponential in the number of
    controllers. That is a fourth obstruction and it is new.
    T5's methodological finding stands and is independent of C1: dt must be swept. Deriving it as
    tau_c/10 put engine.py's own H2b system in a region where the hybrid cannot reach 1% at all,
    while engine.py's hard-coded 0.12 happens to be the optimum -- so its headline win of 560
    against 2046 rested on an unswept constant.

WHAT DOES NOT SURVIVE.
    Every accuracy row in T5/T6/T7 and the whole of T9, because of C1.
    T2's k and cost distributions and T4's edge ledger, because of C3.
    The "residual 2-ball collapses to 96" claim as stated, because of C4.
