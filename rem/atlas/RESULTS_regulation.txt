THE WITHDRAWAL VERDICT IS REVERSED, AND THE REVERSAL IS THE CENSORING COMING OUT. At the 1e-3
threshold -- the level that could actually move a factor-of-two answer -- the count of sensitive
reactions is:

                                    phi=0.0   phi=0.35   phi=0.85
    flux-proportional at optimum       20         24         24
    uniform across all enzymes         20         24         25
    random                             20         24         22

Twenty to twenty-five, across three unrelated regulatory programmes and across a cell forced to
commit 85% of its metabolic proteome to a fixed allocation. The sparsity does NOT depend on the
optimality assumption. G6 passes decisively: the three programmes agree to within five reactions,
so the conclusion does not belong to whichever programme I imposed.

The censored run reported this same quantity as growing 10 -> 40 and printed a withdrawal of
recon.py's central metabolic result. That was the 40-probe limit, not biology.

G5 EXPLAINS WHY BOTH RUNS SAW SOMETHING REAL. At the 1e-9 threshold the set does grow, 28 -> 83 ->
87, and the phi=0 set is CONTAINED in the larger ones at every step -- overlap with phi=0 is
exactly 28 in all three rows. So regulation genuinely adds reactions with nonzero sensitivity, and
it adds them at magnitudes that cannot matter. The count that grows and the count that is flat are
both correct; they are counting different things, and only one of them answers the question.

G2 FAILS, AND THIS LIMITS WHAT MAY BE CLAIMED. At phi = 0 this model finds 28 sensitive reactions
against recon.py's 8, outside the factor-of-two bar, with an overlap of 3/8. The two are not the
same measurement: recon.py varied reaction flux BOUNDS with no proteome budget, while this varies
kcat with a budget and a per-reaction enzyme variable. So the result above is that SPARSITY
SURVIVES REGULATION in an enzyme-constrained model. It is not a confirmation of recon.py's
specific eight, and must not be quoted as one.

The earlier "same sparsity, entirely different reactions" finding was also censoring: the overlap
with recon.py's eight is 3/8 at phi = 0 and 7/8 at phi = 0.35, not the 1/8 first reported.

THE CAVEAT THAT SURVIVES EVERYTHING. Growth changes by 0.002% across the whole sweep while 5,903
of 5,938 floors are active, because the proteome budget's shadow price is zero in this
formulation. The cell is made non-optimal in its ALLOCATION but not in its GROWTH, so this tests
sparsity against misallocation, which is one face of regulation and not the whole of it. A cell
whose regulation actually costs it growth -- through signalling, checkpoints, or a binding budget
-- is still untested.
