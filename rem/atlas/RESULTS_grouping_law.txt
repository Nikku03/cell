  WHY, and this is derivable rather than fitted. For weak dependence with correlation rho:
      MI        = -0.5*log(1 - rho^2)  ~  rho^2 / 2      QUADRATIC in rho
      tail lift ~  rho * (tail z-scores)                 LINEAR   in rho
  A mutual information is an average of log[P_AB/(P_A P_B)] taken under P, and that
  average is second order in the coupling. A tail functional is first order in it. So the
  bulk measure vanishes as the SQUARE of the quantity it is being used to bound, and the
  gap widens without limit as coupling weakens -- which is exactly the regime in which a
  split is ADMITTED.
