R4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
=================================================================================================
  1. A name-matched whitelist is weaker evidence than UniProt's curated DNA_BIND feature.
     The two are tagged separately in the artifact so a consumer can use only the first.
  2. Zinc fingers are the main source of the lift AND the main source of leak; C2H2 arrays
     appear in RNA-binding and protein-interaction roles too.
  3. Coverage is still bounded by UniProt curation. Proteins with no positioned domain of
     any kind remain ineligible however clearly they regulate transcription.
  4. Everything isoedge could not settle is still unsettled: an intact DBD is necessary,
     not sufficient, and the target side of every edge remains unresolved.
