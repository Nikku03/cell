S4  WHAT THE SECTION-14 RULE, AS WRITTEN, ACTUALLY COSTS
=================================================================================================
       threshold          tau   components   largest     state cost
       tail 1e-2    2.443e-07            1        16     6.5536e+04
       tail 1e-3    2.443e-09            1        16     6.5536e+04
       bulk 1e-3    1.000e-03            1        16     6.5536e+04
       bulk 1e-2    1.000e-02           16         1     3.2000e+01
  At the tail-legal threshold the graph is ONE component of 16 variables out of 16.
  The reason is not slow decay -- decay is very fast. It is that MI at distance 1 is 6e-3,
  four orders above tau, so every directly regulated pair is joined; and a regulatory
  network is connected. Thresholding therefore returns the whole network for ANY connected
  topology at ANY tail tolerance, and costs 2^N.
  S4: the literal rule is UNUSABLE at scale -- as predeclared, this is stated plainly

S6  THE TIMESCALE LAW  (this gate was written after seeing a probe)
=================================================================================================
  The original S4 predicted that conditioning on a global controller would restore decay.
  It does not. What follows is why, and when it does.
  The statistic that matters is not the component count -- S4 showed that is always N --
  but the RADIUS r out to which dependence still exceeds tau, because that sets the
  treewidth and hence the cost. It is measured at N = 16, so it can resolve r up to 15;
  at N = 10 it was censored at 9 and said nothing.
     hub rate  tau_hub/tau_gene    I(i;j) d=5   I(i;j|h) d=5     ratio   r_unc  r_cond
        0.001           2000.00    1.4049e-02     1.2436e-08 1129759.3      15       3
        0.002            500.00    1.3996e-02     1.5133e-07   92484.0      15       4
        0.003            333.33    1.3960e-02     3.2807e-07   42552.6      15      12
        0.005            200.00    1.3890e-02     8.7713e-07   15835.4      15      15
        0.010            100.00    1.3716e-02     3.3277e-06    4121.8      15      15
        0.100             10.00    1.1083e-02     1.8692e-04      59.3      15      15
        1.000              1.00    2.8081e-03     8.5090e-04       3.3      15      15
       10.000              0.10    8.1486e-05     6.7431e-05       1.2      15      15
      100.000              0.01    1.0070e-06     9.8707e-07       1.0      15      15
      200.000              0.01    2.7317e-07     2.7054e-07       1.0       5       5
      400.000              0.00    7.9020e-08     7.8667e-08       1.0       4       4
      700.000              0.00    3.1580e-08     3.1507e-08       1.0       3       3
     1000.000              0.00    1.8652e-08     1.8625e-08       1.0       3       3
  conditioning helps most at hub rate 0.0005 (factor 1129759) and least at
  hub rate 1000.0 (factor 1.0).
  MECHANISM. A slow controller is quasi-static, so its current state IS its history and
  conditioning removes everything. A fast controller is white noise on the gene timescale
  and correlates almost nothing, so there is nothing to remove. At MATCHED timescales the
  controller's trajectory carries dependence its instantaneous state does not, and no
  amount of conditioning on that state recovers it. That is the regime a real signalling
  network occupies, and it is the expensive one.
  affordable (r_cond <= 4, cost 2^6 per gene) at hub rates: [0.0005, 0.002, 400.0, 700.0, 1000.0]
  censored at the system size (r_cond >= 15, no grouping helps) at: [0.005, 0.01, 0.1, 1.0, 10.0, 100.0]
  THE WINDOW. Conditioning on a global controller is affordable only when the controller
  is at least ~500x SLOWER than what it controls -- so that its state is its history --
  or at least ~400x FASTER, where it correlates nothing to begin with. Within a factor of
  a few hundred either way, r_cond runs off the end of a 16-gene system and the cost is
  2^N. Kinase signalling at seconds against transcription at tens of minutes sits at
  roughly 100-1000, which is ON that boundary, not comfortably inside it.
  S6: both regimes observed and the boundary is bracketed
