W8  WHAT IS ABSENT
=================================================================================================
  Modelled: metabolism (10,600 reactions), the metabolic proteome, ribosome allocation,
  and the translation cost of the whole proteome including non-metabolic genes.
  NOT modelled, at all:
    - DNA replication and the cell cycle
    - signal transduction and regulation -- the model has no controller
    - splicing, RNA processing, export
    - protein folding, chaperones, quality control
    - secretion, trafficking, the endomembrane system
    - the cytoskeleton and mechanics
    - organelle biogenesis; mitochondria appear only as compartment labels
    - membrane potential and electrochemical gradients beyond stoichiometry
    - every spatial degree of freedom -- the cell is one well-mixed bag
    - cell-to-cell variation; this is one deterministic allocation
  1884 of 19900 genes (9.5%) have a
  mechanism in this model. The other 90.5% appear only as
  a mass and a ribosome burden. That is the honest measure of how much of a cell this is.

=================================================================================================
POST-RUN READING, THIRD RUN (rRNA accounting corrected, sensitivities resolved).
=================================================================================================

A THIRD FRAMING ERROR OF MINE, THE SAME ONE TWICE OVER. Making ribosomes three times cheaper did
not lower the ribosome fraction: growth rose to 0.6486/h and the fraction stayed at 52.9%. The
model spent the saving on more growth and returned to the budget limit. That is not a bug -- at
MAXIMAL growth a high ribosome fraction is what the answer looks like, which is the Scott-Hwa
growth law. The printed comparison "mammalian cells are a few per cent" is the same category error
as the doubling-time prediction: a few per cent is what a slow-growing REGULATED cell shows, and
this model has no regulator. None of this model's absolute numbers is a claim about a human cell.
They answer "what is the fastest this constraint set permits, and what binds".

THE SENSITIVITIES ARE NOW REAL. With the bisection at 1e-9 and a 5% perturbation the resolution
floor is 3.16e-08, and every value sits above it with the right sign -- a faster enzyme now
increases growth, which the previous run's -0.000542 could not:

    k_elong, ribosome speed                    +0.977180
    proteome budget                            +0.038796
    k_dp of the non-metabolic proteome         -0.010216
    kcat DHORD9  (the largest of any enzyme)   +0.000775
    kcat OMPDC                                 +0.000310
    the other six probed enzymes               +0.000029 to +0.000086

Ribosome speed outranks the most important single enzyme in human metabolism by a factor of 1,261.
The whole-cell answer is set by ALLOCATION.

W7 IS NOW WORTH READING, because the model is demonstrably responsive rather than stuck. Dividing
every catalytic rate by ten costs 26% of growth; doubling ribosome speed gives 1.941 and halving
it 0.501. Across all eight variants the binding limitation is translation capacity every time.

THE THREE SUBSYSTEM RESULTS, TOGETHER:

    metabolism   recon.py     8 of 5,938 enzyme reactions have a realised sensitivity, because
                              97.7% of capacity constraints do not bind
    expression   expression.py  every gene keeps free directions under abundance assays, so K
                              grows with the genome -- unless a degradation rate is measured,
                              which closes the rank exactly and takes K to zero
    coupled      here         allocation dominates every individual rate by three orders, and the
                              non-metabolic proteome's DEGRADATION rate is the third-ranked
                              parameter in the whole model

The sign on that third term is the closure. Destabilising non-metabolic protein REDUCES growth,
because it must be resynthesised continuously and that competes for the ribosome capacity growth
needs. Degradation enters through exactly the constraint that binds. expression.py proved by exact
linear algebra that degradation rates are what RNA-seq, proteomics and ribosome profiling together
cannot see at any precision. Two independent routes, one quantity.
