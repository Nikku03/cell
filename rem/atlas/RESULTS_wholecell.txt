W8  WHAT IS ABSENT
=================================================================================================
  Modelled: metabolism (10,600 reactions), the metabolic proteome, ribosome allocation,
  and the translation cost of the whole proteome including non-metabolic genes.
  NOT modelled, at all:
    - DNA replication and the cell cycle
    - signal transduction and regulation -- the model has no controller
    - splicing, RNA processing, export
    - protein folding, chaperones, quality control
    - secretion, trafficking, the endomembrane system
    - the cytoskeleton and mechanics
    - organelle biogenesis; mitochondria appear only as compartment labels
    - membrane potential and electrochemical gradients beyond stoichiometry
    - every spatial degree of freedom -- the cell is one well-mixed bag
    - cell-to-cell variation; this is one deterministic allocation
  1884 of 19900 genes (9.5%) have a
  mechanism in this model. The other 90.5% appear only as
  a mass and a ribosome burden. That is the honest measure of how much of a cell this is.

=================================================================================================
POST-RUN READING.  Hand-written after the run.
=================================================================================================

THE MODEL RUNS AND THE COUPLING IS REAL, BUT IT SITS IN A DEGENERATE CORNER AND THE GROWTH RATE
IS NOT BIOLOGICAL.  W4 passes decisively -- plain FBA gives 370.4/h, the coupled model 1.087/h, a
factor of 340 -- so expression genuinely constrains metabolism and this is not FBA with extra
steps.  W1, W2 and W3 pass.  But three numbers together condemn the parameterisation:

    ribosome mass fraction of the metabolic proteome   0.9993
    doubling time                                      0.64 h
    every probed kcat sensitivity                      0.006172, identical to six decimals

A cell that is 99.93% ribosome is not a cell, a human cell does not double in 38 minutes, and
eight unrelated enzymes cannot share a sensitivity to six decimal places unless they are all on
one degenerate face of the linear program.

THE CAUSE IS A UNIT ERROR, AND IT IS MINE.  kcat was set to 25/s = 90,000/h while the enzyme
variable E lives in a budget normalised to 1.0.  So v_j <= 90,000 * E_j lets any flux be bought
with almost no protein, enzymes become free, and the optimiser spends the entire budget on
ribosome because translation is then the only binding constraint.  The model did not discover that
growth is ribosome-limited; it was told so by a mis-scaled constant.

W7 THEREFORE PASSES FOR THE WRONG REASON, and its pass must not be quoted.  The limiting layer is
"translation capacity" in all seven variants -- kcat x10, kcat /10, budget x2, ribosome speed x2,
and both protein fractions -- which looks like robustness and is actually a model that cannot
leave one corner.  Note kcat x10 and kcat /10 change growth by 0.06% and 0.6%: enzymes are so far
from binding that a hundredfold change in every catalytic rate in human metabolism does almost
nothing.  That is the signature of the error, visible in the sweep that was supposed to catch it.

WHAT THE CORRECT SCALING GIVES, worked through before rerunning.  In mass units: total protein
about 0.5 g/gDW; average protein 50 kDa; a ribosome is 3.2 MDa and elongates at about 5.5 aa/s,
which is 0.68 g of protein per g of ribosome per hour.  Sustaining a proteome at growth mu then
needs a ribosome mass fraction of roughly (mu + k_dp) * 0.5 / 0.68.  At mu = 1/h that demands more
ribosome than there is protein -- infeasible, which is the model correctly saying a human cell
cannot double in an hour.  At a human doubling time near 24 h, mu = 0.029/h, the ribosome fraction
comes out near 6%, which is the right order for a mammalian cell.

So the fix is not a tuning knob: it is that flux, enzyme and ribosome must be carried in one
consistent unit system, with capacity written v <= kcat * E / MW.  The rerun follows, and the
growth rate it must reproduce -- roughly 0.03/h rather than 1.09/h -- is a prediction this reading
makes before the numbers exist.
