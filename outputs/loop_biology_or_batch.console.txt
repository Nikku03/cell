LOOP 269 -- IS THE LEARNED OPERATOR CELL BIOLOGY, OR IS IT BATCH?
========================================================================================================
     Everything in this arc rests on W_c. Loop 255 measured why that is dangerous:
     on LINCS, profiles sharing a PLATE correlated at 0.7788, against a construct
     ceiling of 0.2487 and a random baseline of 0.2683 -- batch structure three times
     larger than the biological ceiling. If lines had their own plates, W_c would be
     a batch effect wearing a cell's name.
     Tahoe can settle it and LINCS could not: 8 plates, 50 lines, every plate carries
     all 50. A property of the CELL survives a change of plate. A property of the
     BATCH does not.
V1 ARE PLATE AND LINE ACTUALLY CROSSED?
     49 cell lines, 8 plates; plates per line min 7, max 8
     V1 PASS -- every line is on at least 7 plates, so plate is a control axis and not confounded with the variable under test
V2 HOW LARGE IS THE PLATE EFFECT HERE?
     drug 20.0%, line 7.8%, plate 6.3%
     LINCS plate correlation was 0.7788 against a 0.2487 construct ceiling
     V2 PASS -- plate accounts for 6.3%, less than drug or line
     plate halves: A=['1', '2', '3', '4']  B=['5', '6', '7', '8']
     operator on K=169 genes, ~339 conditions per line per plate-half (2.0 obs per parameter)
     fitted 2 x 49 operators   [12s]
V3 LOAD-BEARING -- DOES THE OPERATOR SURVIVE A CHANGE OF PLATE?
     own-line operator from plates A, scored on the same line's plates B: +0.0283
     (baseline on those conditions 0.4413)
     V3 PASS -- the operator is worth +0.0283 on plates it was never fitted on, so it describes the cell outside its own batch
V4 IS IT MORE A PROPERTY OF THE LINE THAN OF THE PLATE?
     within-line across-plate  +0.1820
     across-line same-plate    -0.0220
     difference +0.2041
     V4 PASS -- the same line across different plates is +0.2041 more similar than different lines on the same plate; the operator tracks the CELL
V5 DOES THE OWN-LINE OPERATOR BEAT ANOTHER LINE'S ON HELD-OUT PLATES?
     own line +0.0283 vs another line's operator -0.0371   +0.0654
     both fitted on plate-half A, both scored on plate-half B, same estimator
     V5 PASS -- the own-line operator is worth +0.0654 over another line's on the same held-out plates
V6 WHAT THIS CANNOT SHOW
     Plate is the only batch variable in this table. Run date, passage number,
     confluence and operator are not recorded here, so 'survives a plate change' is
     not 'survives every technical factor'.
     A PASS says the operator is a stable property of the cell line as cultured in
     this laboratory. It does not say the operator is MECHANISM: loops 263 and 264
     showed it aligns with no pathway, no interaction graph and no annotation tested.
     Stable and unexplained are compatible.
     50 cancer lines, one platform, drug perturbations. Nothing here extends to
     primary cells or to genetic perturbation.
     Everything is on the double-centred residual, so by loop 260 any arm constant
     across lines is pinned near zero however expressive it is.
