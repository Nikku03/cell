     comparable; only the sign and rough size of the off-diagonal effect are.
     DRUGS, not gene knockdowns. A drug hits many targets and its 'perturbation
     identity' is not a gene, so agreement with LINCS is a statement about response
     structure, not about the same biological intervention.

     this curve is almost certainly still climbing at its right edge and U5 says so.
     50 cancer cell lines on one platform. Saturation within that population is not
