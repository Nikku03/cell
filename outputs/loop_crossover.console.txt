     added line, so every number in the borrow column is a floor that more lines
     would raise, and the crossover would move RIGHT with more lines.
     The evaluation half is fixed per line but the measured subset is drawn from the
     other half only, so the largest budget is half a line's genes and no budget
     above that is tested.
     Everything is on the double-centred residual, so by loop 260 any arm constant
