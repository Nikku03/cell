T7 WHAT THIS CANNOT SHOW
     Fitness is a SCALAR per (gene, line). There is no response profile and no
     operator, so nothing here speaks to loop 262's off-diagonal finding, which was
     about coupling BETWEEN readout genes and needs a multivariate readout.
     The measured genes are a RANDOM subset, as in loop 266. A chosen panel would
     almost certainly need fewer genes, and nothing here says which to choose.
     908 lines is many, but they are all CANCER cell lines from 27 types. Saturation
     here is saturation within that population, not across human cell states.
     CRISPR knockout is not shRNA knockdown, and a fitness readout is not expression.
     Agreement with LINCS would be a real cross-assay result; disagreement would not
     by itself refute either.
     Everything is on the double-centred residual, so by loop 260 and T2 any arm
     constant across lines is pinned near zero however expressive it is.
